# Symbolic transformation rule coverage ledger

This ledger maps every reflected method-level transformation rule to discriminating behavioral coverage. The reflection inventory is the change-detection guard; it is not behavioral evidence. Coverage modes distinguish `PUBLIC DISPATCH` (the public transformer selects the rule), `DIRECT RULE BODY` (a focused protected-rule invocation), `STRUCTURAL AST`, and `NEGATIVE DISPATCH`. `DUPLICATE_DISPATCH` records indistinguishable production registrations rather than claiming independent public reachability.

## Audit note

`PowerConvertionNumber1` and `PowerConversionMath` both advertise `Call:System.Double.Pow`; they are indistinguishable to method-level dispatch. Their bodies are tested directly, but both are marked `DUPLICATE_DISPATCH`: direct invocation cannot establish independent public reachability. This duplicate registration requires a future production cleanup and is intentionally unchanged here. The integration `PowerMathCall` overloads are tested through public `Integrate` dispatch with genuine `System.Math.Pow` nodes, covering both normal and minus-one branches for constant and converted exponents.
