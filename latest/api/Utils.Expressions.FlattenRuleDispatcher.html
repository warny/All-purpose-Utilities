Delegate FlattenRuleDispatcher

Namespace
Utils.Expressions
Assembly
Utils.dll

Applies to one node the signature rules (ExpressionSignatureAttribute) that the group of a chain covers, through the dispatch of the transformer that handed it to a FlattenAttribute rule: the candidates of the node's type in the transformer's plan, in their declaration order, with the same signature matching, parameter checks, admission, budgets, exception contract and tracing as the node-by-node traversal.

public delegate bool FlattenRuleDispatcher(Expression node, out Expression? result)

Parameters

node Expression

A prepared node of an operator the chain's group declares (its direct or inverse operation, or one of its unary operators): a framework BinaryExpression or UnaryExpression whose operands the rule has already transformed as it chose. The dispatcher prepares nothing: it neither transforms the operands again, nor extracts a chain from the node, nor calls a flatten rule.

result Expression

The result of the first rule that produced one. A signature rule of the single-node or Expression[] shape may produce null, as it does in the traversal; ExpressionTransformer keeps that result as is.

Returns

bool

true when a rule produced result; false when no candidate applies, every candidate declines, or the transformer's effect gate refuses the node. In that case the node is unchanged and is not finalized: the flatten rule decides what to do with it.

Remarks

Each call counts one transformation visit and checks the execution stack, like the treatment of a node by the traversal; inside a tracing scope it is a treatment of its own, requested by the running flatten rule. The rules a dispatched node runs may call TransformCore on new nodes: those calls are ordinary transformations, which may root chains of their own.

A dispatcher is bound to one transformer instance and one group. It may be kept and called later: outside a running operation it starts one with the limits of its transformer, as RebuildFlattenedChain does.
