Fields

DigitByDigit = 1

Every decimal digit is read after the decimal separator word, a 0 with the language's standalone zero (zéro virgule zéro zéro zéro un), even when a <Fraction> is configured and whatever the language's fallback limit.

LanguageDefault = 0

The language decides: the configured <Fraction> for the number of decimal digits when there is one (vingt et un virgule cinq centièmes), otherwise the digits one by one when the language allows it at that precision (decimalDigitByDigitMaxDigits, unlimited when absent), otherwise NotSupportedException.

MathematicalFraction = 2

The decimal part is read as a numerator over the named denominator 10^n of its n digits, without the decimal separator word: the fraction alone for a zero integer part (un dix-millième), joined to a non-zero integer part by the language's mixedFractionSeparator (deux et un dix-millième). A zero decimal part is not read. Throws NotSupportedException when the language names no denominator for that digit count, or declares no mixed-number connector and the integer part is not zero.
