Remarks

Unlike ScaleForms, which holds whole words for one exact scale index, the forms here are suffix fragments (Russian он / она / онов): the converter selects a key with Selector from the group multiplier, then builds the noun with the Conway prefix machinery of the scale and the selected fragment (три + лли + она). An exact scale form, when one is configured for the same scale, keeps precedence.

A form may also carry MultiplierForcedVariants: the variant values the noun imposes on the numeral of its multiplier when that form is selected (Czech dvě biliardy: the feminine few form forces gender on dva). They are overlaid on the caller's variants, forced values winning dimension by dimension, and that query drives the whole local pipeline of the group (group triggers, variant rules of the multiplier, onScale variant rules, group-with-scale triggers); the group is then left untouched by the whole-number passes run with the caller's query. The selector runs once, before they are applied. Instances are immutable.

Parameters

forms LexicalFormSet

The suffix fragment of each form key; must not be empty.

selector ILexicalFormSelector

The selector choosing the form key from the group multiplier, or null for DefaultLexicalFormSelector (keys "singular" and "plural").

multiplierForcedVariants IReadOnlyDictionary<string, ForcedVariantSet>

The variants each form key forces on the multiplier, or null for none. Every key must be a key of forms. Validated against the language when the converter is built.
