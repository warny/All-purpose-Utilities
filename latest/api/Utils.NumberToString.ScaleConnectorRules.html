The connector between the multiplier of a scale group and its scale noun (NTS-53): a default, then the contextual Substitutions that differ from it. Romanian, after the Academy grammar (GALR 2005): de from twenty (douăzeci de mii, o sută douăzeci de mii) except after a multiplier whose last two digits are one to nineteen (o sută nouăsprezece mii).

Remarks

For each group, the substitutions whose constraints are all satisfied are collected; the one with the greatest Specificity wins and, at equal specificity, the first declared wins (the declaration order is authoritative, never an ambiguity error). With no match, the legacy ScaleConnector shorthand applies when its threshold is reached (it acts as the last declared substitution, on numberRange="threshold.."), and the default otherwise.

A group is assembled as multiplier + Separator + connector + Separator + scale when the selected connector is not empty, otherwise with the historical multiplier/scale junction. Instances are immutable; the substitutions are copied in the order supplied.
