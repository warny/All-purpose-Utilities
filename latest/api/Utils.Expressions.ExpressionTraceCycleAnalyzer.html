Looks for non-terminating rewriting in trace events (see ExpressionTraceCollector): forms re-entered inside their own treatment, periodic rule sequences along the deepest recursion, and rules that never reduce the forms. A diagnostic tool, separate from the engine: it never transforms, simplifies or canonicalizes anything, and never changes how the engine behaves.

Remarks

Strict structural identity. Forms are identified by a structural fingerprint that respects operand order, node types, result types, methods and members, constant values (primitive types, decimal, string, char, Boolean and enumerations by value, everything else by reference) and parameters by reference, so a scope or a lambda parameter is never confused with another one; extension nodes are taken by reference without reading any of their members. Mathematical equivalence is never taken for identity: a + b and b + a are different forms. A reported recurrence is confirmed with the engine's raw structural comparison (Confirmed).

Recursion levels. Recurrences and periods are searched along the chain of nested treatments (parent and child treatments, whether a child was prepared by its parent or requested by a rule), not only between consecutive events, so a loop that goes through several rules and several levels of recursion is found.

Resources. Every fingerprint walk runs as an engine-internal structural walk: it counts structural visits and checks the stack, inside the running operation or a default-limited one. Do not call the analyzer from a tracer callback, which refuses engine operations.

Scope of the conclusions. The analyzer searches the events it is given for cycles and suspicious behaviors; it is not a termination proof. An empty report says that this trace shows no recurrence or period, not that the rule system terminates on other inputs, and a trace truncated by a collector or by UndeliveredEvents may hide a period.
