Remarks

Returns form keys only; the words are configured in XML (<ScaleForm>):

  • "one" — the last digit is 1 and the last two digits are not 11 (1, 21, 101: тысяча);
  • "few" — the last digit is 2-4 and the last two digits are not 12-14 (2, 22, 104: тысячи);
  • "many" — every other integer, including 0 and 11-14 (5, 11, 12, 100: тысяч).

Source: Unicode CLDR, "Language Plural Rules", ru (i % 10 = 1 and i % 100 != 11; i % 10 = 2..4 and i % 100 != 12..14). The rule only depends on the absolute value; the variants are ignored. Stateless and thread-safe.
