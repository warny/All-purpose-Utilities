ArabicOrdinalLanguageSpecifics

Produces Arabic ordinals above nineteen, in the same indefinite short nominative form without the article as the configured ordinals 1–19. From 21 the unit is an ordinal agreeing in gender and the tens stay cardinal, joined by و (حادٍ وعشرون, feminine حادية وعشرون); round tens, one hundred, and one thousand use the cardinal word, which is the ordinal form in Modern Standard Arabic (العشرون, المائة, الألف with the article).

ArabicScaleLexicalFormSelector

Chooses the form of an Arabic scale noun (e.g. ألف, "thousand") from its multiplier, following the rule of the counted noun (تمييز العدد): the noun agrees with the last number written.

BasqueOrdinalLanguageSpecifics

Basque ordinal domain. Euskaltzaindia, rule 18: the ordinal is the cardinal written in letters followed by -garren (bigarren, bosgarren, hogeita batgarren, ehungarren, milagarren …). NTS-08 validated 1–1000; NTS-28 extends the rule to the NTS-27 cardinal (rule 7: mila eta batgarren, milioi batgarren), to 999 999 999. Zero has no attested ordinal (rule 7 gives only zero/huts), and from the milliard the cardinal does not compose mila milioi with the millions (NTS-30); those values fail closed.

BulgarianOrdinalLanguageSpecifics

Produces Bulgarian ordinals. Only the last element of a compound is ordinal and, as in the cardinal, the conjunction и precedes that last element (двадесет и първи, сто и първи, хиляда сто и втори). Ordinals agree in gender: the masculine citation form (първи, стотен) is used for the default standalone value and for masculine; feminine and neuter derive -а/-о (първа, първо) and -на/-но (стотна, стотно).

ChineseOrdinalLanguageSpecifics

Chinese ordinal domain: the prefix 第 before the cardinal (國語辭典: "用於整數數詞之前。表事物的順序或等級", before any integer numeral). NTS-08 validated 0–100 and the round hundreds; NTS-28 extends it to the NTS-27 cardinal domain with 零 and 一十, counted by 万 (below 万亿, 10^12).

CroatianOrdinalLanguageSpecifics

Restricts Croatian ordinals to verified forms. Croatian ordinals are produced by the declarative XML rules (only the last component is ordinal: tisuću prvi, tri tisuće sedamsto trideset treći), and the single scale units have lexical ordinals (tisućiti, milijunti, milijarditi). Every other round scale value, i.e. any multiple of 1000 other than the verified lexical units 1 000, 1 000 000 and 1 000 000 000 (2000., 21 000., 2 000 000., 10^12., ...), would end with an inflected scale noun whose ordinal form was not verified, so this plugin rejects it instead of letting a cardinal or invented form through.

DutchOrdinalLanguageSpecifics

Dutch ordinal domain (NTS-08). Taaladvies "Aaneenschrijven van telwoorden" and the Woordenlijst Nederlandse Taal (nulde, tweeduizendste) validate the ordinals from 0 to 999 999. NTS-31 extends them to every magnitude up to MaxValue (MinValue is rejected by the engine, its absolute value not being representable): the e-ANS (rangtelwoorden) gives the scale ordinals (miljoenste, miljardste) and the Besluit bekendmaking spellingvoorschriften 2005, rule 6.N, the compounds (twee miljoenste, twee miljoen tweehonderdduizendste): the cardinal keeps its spelling and only its final element becomes ordinal, which the declarative pipeline forms on every value. The guard therefore rejects nothing; it keeps the validated domain explicit.

FinnishOrdinalLanguageSpecifics

Finnish ordinal domain: the nominative of 0–1 999 999. NTS-08 validated 0–20, the round tens and hundreds and 1000 (Aalto University "Numeroiden taivutus", Kielitoimisto; nollas, NTS-12). NTS-28 added the compounds, one word with the ordinal marker in every part (Kielitoimiston ohjepankki: viideskymmenesviides, kahdeskymmenesensimmäinen). NTS-37 opened the thousands and the first million, composed by the configuration: the marker in every part, yhdes/kahdes inside a larger number, one space between groups (Kielitoimiston ohjepankki: 3 760. kolmastuhannes seitsemässadaskuudeskymmenes, 1 000 000:s miljoonas).

FormVariantType

A variant node inside a form-producing element (<Replacement>, <OrdinalException>, <Ordinal>). Plays two roles depending on whether Forms is present:

  • Intermediate node DimensionType + VariantValue add one constraint; child <Variant> elements cascade further constraints.
  • Leaf node DimensionType + Forms provide one output form per dimension value in the order declared by the matching <Dimension> element. Empty entries are skipped.

All (constraints, form) pairs are collected at load time and merged by constraint set into OrdinalVariantRule or VariantRule entries — no runtime changes required.

GalicianOrdinalLanguageSpecifics

HindiOrdinalLanguageSpecifics

Hindi ordinal domain. HungarianOrdinalLanguageSpecifics

Produces Hungarian ordinals. The ordinal suffix attaches to the last morpheme of the numeral with vowel harmony and stem changes (tizenegyedik, huszadik, századik, ezredik, milliomodik); only the numbers 1 and 2 standing alone are suppletive (első, második), whereas they are regular at the end of a compound (huszonegyedik, tizenkettedik). Numbers up to 2000 are written as one word and larger ones separate their thousand groups with hyphens (kétezer-egyedik); a single million or milliard is written with egy (egymilliomodik, egymillió-egyedik).

RussianOrdinalLanguageSpecifics

Russian ordinal domain, after the Правила русской орфографии и пунктуации (2006). §133б: a composite ordinal is written separately and only its last part is ordinal (тысяча девятьсот девяносто четвёртый, две тысячи первый, миллион двести тысяч сто пятый); the declarative pipeline forms them on the NTS-27 cardinal (the configuration drops the один of a single million). §132б: an ordinal whose last part is -тысячный or -миллионный is one word with its first part in the genitive (пятнадцатитысячный, шестисотмиллионный); this class builds them, declined for gender and case, also after the millions (два миллиона трёхтысячный).

SlovakOrdinalLanguageSpecifics

Produces Slovak ordinals. Tens and units are ordinal (dvadsiaty prvý); hundreds and thousands of a compound stay cardinal, fused with a following unit (stoprvý, dvetisícdruhý) and separate before tens (päťsto dvadsiaty ôsmy). SpanishOrdinalLanguageSpecifics

Spanish ordinal domain. Validated by the RAE/ASALE Diccionario panhispánico de dudas, "ordinales" (NTS-08 for 1–29, the round tens, 100 and 1000; NTS-28 for the rest): §1 table and §2–§3, the complex ordinals juxtapose the simple ones (trigésimo primero, centésimo trigésimo cuarto), the round hundreds are simple (ducentésimo), the thousands and millions prefix the multiplying cardinal (dosmilésimo, quinientosmilésimo, dosmillonésimo). The configuration composes them; this guard admits 1–999 999 999 except the round thousands and millions whose multiplier the DPD does not show soldered: a multiplier ending in uno (21, 31, 101 …, apocope undetermined), a multiplier written in several words (150 ciento cincuenta) and, in the feminine, a hundreds multiplier (doscientas). Zero and the milliard and above (whose cardinal is not composed, NTS-30) fail closed. A composed value is checked part by part, so a rejected round part rejects the whole ordinal.
