Describes how the ordinal of a round scale value is formed: a value whose highest non-zero group is at a scale covered by Scales and whose lower groups are all zero (multiplier × scale unit, e.g. 2 000 000 = 2 × million).

Remarks

The ordinal is then built from the scale noun rather than from the assembled cardinal: a multiplier of one is dropped (the singular scale noun alone), a larger multiplier is its own cardinal, rendered exactly like the standalone cardinal of the multiplier with the caller's variants (cardinal variant rules included, onValue evaluated against the multiplier), followed by MultiplierSeparator and the singular scale noun. That text then goes through the usual ordinal transformation (ordinal replacements, word rules, stems, removeTrailing, the effective suffix of the selected variant). Whole-number ordinal exceptions keep precedence.

A value is matched at its highest scale only: 10^9 is the ordinal of the billion scale, never a thousand times the million scale.

Scales string

The scale indices covered (1 = thousands, 2 = the next scale …), in the range syntax of ParseRangeExpression(string) (e.g. "2..4"). Every index must be at least 1 and nameable by the converter's scale; two rules must not cover the same index.

MultiplierSeparator string

The text between the multiplier and the scale noun; may be empty.
