P19d — Re-evaluation of the inert-classification memo

Status: studied, not kept (2026-11-12). No production change; the prototype stays on a local experiment branch.

This note answers the question left open by SimplificationPolicies.md §9.7 and ArchitectureC-Audit.md §8.4: now that P19d (#688) has removed most of the comparer probe work, is the per-operation memo of IsInertArithmetic, withdrawn at C2b, worth shipping?

1. What was measured

Variant Source Content
A 6a14a78c (master c027aa7f + the P19d review finalization tests, PR #689, squash-merged as e64c0ba1) base, no memo
B a copy of A's binaries A/A control (noise of the protocol)
C cefdb53d, experiment branch experiment/s5-p19d-inert-memo (not pushed) the C2b memo ported onto A
D C with INERT_MEMO_DIAGNOSTICS defined classification counters; never timed

The prototype is the C2b patch of §9.7 (Dictionary<Expression, bool> keyed by reference identity, held by the ExpressionResourceBudget session, intermediate and negative results stored, nodes whose classification reads user-suppliable metadata left uncached), with two deliberate differences:

  • the memoized walk counts no structural visit, like the historical walk, so budgets and refusals cannot differ from A;
  • the diagnostics counters exist only under INERT_MEMO_DIAGNOSTICS, so the timed build C carries no counter.

2. Exactness and work (deterministic)

Growth harness, 11 families (N = nanpow, U, A, B, K, C, D, E, G, V, M3), n = 8, 16, 32, 64, 128, 256, policies default, aggressive, trust and permissive: 260 rows per variant. A case whose first, unlimited simplification takes more than 2 s is recorded as stopped, without measured columns, and its policy is skipped for the larger n of the family; the 25 stopped rows are the same for A, C and D (n = 256 of A, K, M3, N, V under the four policies, of B under the default, and n = 128 of G under the four policies). On the 235 measured rows, results, result sizes, transform and structural visits, comparer simplifications, verdict under the default limits and opaque type reads are identical for A, C and D.

The memo still makes the walk linear. The walk is not counted among the structural visits, so the two figures measure different traversals; as a ratio of traversal volumes, the nodes the walk examines are about 1 % of the structural visits at n = 64 (8,256 / 805,155) and 0.5 % at n = 128 (32,896 / 6,365,763). This is not a measure of CPU time (a walk step and a structural visit do not cost the same); §3 measures the time directly. The volume of nanpow is elsewhere: per doubling of n, structural visits grow ×7.8 (≈ n³) and comparer simplifications are exactly 2n² + 2n (144, 544, 2,112, 8,320, 33,024 at n = 8 to 128). That is the remaining comparer-probe term of S5A-14/S5A-15 on this family, which P19d reduced by a constant factor (907 → 66 ms at n = 64) but not in order. The default policy never reaches the memo path and is unaffected by it.

3. Timing (BenchmarkDotNet)

BenchmarkDotNet 0.14.0, in-process, 8 warmups, 20 iterations, memory diagnoser, affinity 0x14, high priority, one process per variant and round, three rounds in rotated order (A, B, C / B, C, A / C, A, B), median of the round means. Round 0 and the B run of round 1 were taken on 2026-11-11, the other runs on 2026-11-12 (the campaign was interrupted by a shutdown). The memo is therefore always compared with a base measured the same day; only the A/A pair of round 1 spans the two days. No case gains beyond the A/A spread, nanpow included: at n = 128 the variants are within 0.2 % (485 ms), with memo deltas of +0.1 / +3.8 / −2.7 % per round. This absence of gain is the robust result.

The time costs are less stable than at C2b. Per round (memo vs base, rounds 0 / 1 / 2):

Case memo vs base per round
permissive / aggressive NaN + x ** y +4.4 / +10.5 / −1.4 %, +5.5 / +9.8 / +3.1 %
permissive U64 / U256 +8.8 / +10.6 / +0.2 %, +7.1 / +8.5 / +0.3 %

Rounds 0 and 1 reproduce the C2b costs, round 2, measured entirely on 2026-11-12, does not. The +215 B of the table on small operands is deterministic; the time costs (about +150 ns on NaN + x ** y, +5 to +9 % on the uncacheable trees) are likely but not established by this campaign. Dropping the counted visits is not expected to remove them, since they come from the table and the lookups, not from the budget.

Raw data. The BenchmarkDotNet reports of the nine runs (and, for the 2026-11-12 runs, the logs and per-iteration measurements), the growth tables, the bench program, the scripts with the exact commands, and the prototype as a git am patch are archived locally as p19d-inert-memo-raw-2026-11-12.zip (484,099 bytes, SHA-256 1228F1B3499A1C3F83F2794F63C9B8D796B5E8A925B1894EDA9B78754F521AE6), with a README listing the variants, the build and run commands and the stopped cases. It is not committed: the repository keeps no measurement data.

P19d divided the surrounding work by about 16, yet the walk's share stays negligible because the surrounding work is still super-quadratic; the medians of the memo's costs on small and uncacheable trees are of the same order as at C2b (with the round-to-round instability noted above).

4. Decision

Following the decision rule of the study (keep only a net benefit with no regression on small trees or memory): the memo is not shipped, the result matches C2b. The decision rests on the absence of any gain and on the deterministic +215 B per small operand; it does not depend on the time costs, which this campaign does not establish firmly. IsInertArithmetic stays an uncounted, unmemoized walk; the branch experiment/s5-p19d-inert-memo is kept locally for reference only and is not to be merged.

Redoing the study only makes sense if the comparer probes of nanpow (≈ n³ structural visits, 2n² + 2n comparer simplifications) become linear or close to it; that is an S5A-14/S5A-15 follow-up, outside the scope of C3 and of this note. The harness family nanpow stays for that purpose.
