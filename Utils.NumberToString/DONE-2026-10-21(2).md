# NTS-47 — last audit of NTS-42: Slovak generated *-ón* genitive, D and E/CS (2026-10-21)

Starting master: `d11521b8` (NTS-46, #653; NTS-45 #652 and NTS-36/42 F #651 present). NTS-42 kept three closures after
NTS-46: D (CS/SK *lomeno*/*lomené* under an external case), E/CS (Czech zero in an oblique case) and the Slovak genitive
singular of a generated *-ón* scale noun. This change opens the third on new sources and closes the first two as
deliberately unsupported, which **closes NTS-42**. One production change: a configuration key (plus source comments in
the CS and SK configurations). No C#, public API, XSD, interface or engine change. Sources:
`docs/NTS-08-linguistic-sources.md`, section "NTS-47" (#1–#11 below refer to it); permanent reference for the two
deliberate closures: `docs/NTS-42-deliberate-closures.md`.

| Family | Result |
|---|---|
| SK — genitive singular of a generated *-ón* noun | **implemented** |
| D/CS, D/SK — *lomeno*/*lomené* under an external case (SK zero + slash included) | **closed, deliberately unsupported** — grammatical construction not established |

## A. D — *lomeno*/*lomené* under an external case (deliberately unsupported)

**Question.** When a whole slash phrase *X lomeno Y* / *X lomené Y* (a denominator without a fraction noun: 2/23, 1/142,
1/10^12) is governed by a preposition or a case, is the first operand declined (*s dvěma lomeno dvaceti třemi*, *s dvoma
lomené dvadsiatimi troma*) or kept in its absolute arithmetic form (*s dvě lomeno …*, *s dva lomené …*)?

**Sources checked.**
- SSJČ, *lomiti*: *dvě lomeno třemi*, the mathematical "divided by" (NTS-40/41) — isolated phrase.
- E. Prandstetter, "Čtení matematických značek a výrazů", *Naše řeč* 22 (1938) — internal structure *X lomeno
  Y(instr.)* only.
- J. Sláma, "Operátor lomeno, koordinátor lomeno, adordinátor: K funkcím slova lomeno", *Naše řeč* 105 (2022), 49–55
  (#6): **only the abstract could be read from the project environment** (CEEOL paywall; ResearchGate refused the
  request; the Naše řeč archive gives no PDF for 2022). The abstract lists the functions of *lomeno* (name of the sign,
  mathematical operator, coordinator 'or', adordinator) and says nothing about the case of the operands; the reviewer of
  this change read the full text on ResearchGate and found no rule for the first operand under an external case. Its
  status of operator is not an answer to the question.
- ČVUT FJFI, Mathematics I, lesson 9 (2024, #11): *a/x — a lomeno x*, isolated.
- ÚJČ poradna database (#7): no public answer for *lomeno*.
- Slovak: P. Ondrus, *Slovenská reč* 1968/2; Š. Peciar, *Slovenská reč* 1976/4; *Slovenská reč* 1978/6 (NTS-45) —
  *jedna lomené troma*, *jeden lomené sto štyridsiatimi dvoma*, isolated.

**Verdict.** No source shows a whole slash phrase under an external case. The uncertainty is **syntactic** (does the
arithmetic term take the external case at all?), not morphological (*dvěma*, *dvoma* exist). Neither the existence of
the instrumental of *dva* nor the absolute form of the isolated reading is evidence. The research has reached what can
reasonably be sourced: **closed as deliberately unsupported — grammatical construction not established**; the code is
unchanged (`NotSupportedException`) and the CS and SK configurations state the closure next to their language
specifics.

**Tests.** `NumberToStringNts47Tests.D_SlashExternalCase_DeliberatelyUnsupported` (CS 2/23, 1/21; SK 2/23, 1/142;
genitive, dative, accusative, locative, instrumental; nominatives unchanged),
`D_GeneratedSlashAndSlovakZero_DeliberatelyUnsupported` (CS and SK
1/10^12, SK 1/10^18 — the new genitive key does not open D — and SK 0/23), `D_FractionNounIsNotFamilyD` (2/3 has a
fraction noun in both languages and reads in every case since NTS-40: it is not D). The characterization tests of
`NumberToStringNts42Tests` (D) are unchanged.

## B. E/CS — Czech zero in an oblique case (deliberately unsupported)

- **Lexical paradigm**: *nula* is a feminine noun, pattern *žena* (*nuly, nule, nulu, nulou*; IJP, ASCS, SSČ, SSJČ,
  NTS-45); the ÚJČ poradna calls it "obecné podstatné jméno" (#7, answer #7437).
- **Cardinal use**: SSJČ, *nula*, sense 2 "čísl. označuje počet žádných jednotek" (#10); CzechEncy *teplota nula
  stupňů* (#8).
- **Academic morphology — both behaviours**: NovaMorf (Osolsobě, Hlaváčová, Petkevič, Šimandl, Svášek; #9, § 3.1.1)
  treats *nula* as a cardinal in its quantitative uses and attests *záruka na nula/nulu vadných bodů*: the declined and
  the indeclinable form in one context; its model gives *nula* "kromě skloňovaných tvarů sg. pro všechny pády i
  nesklonný tvar (*do nula celá sedmi*)".
- **Usage**: an indeclinable quantifier occurs (*při nula stupních*, NTS-45; *s nula stupni volnosti* in a VUT thesis
  cited by the request, not re-read here).
- **No canonical form**: neither the IJP ("Počítaný předmět po číslovkách": *nula* as a quantifier in the nominative
  only), the ÚJČ poradna database (#7), CzechEncy (#8) nor NovaMorf (#9) prefers one behaviour, and no source gives the
  fraction paradigm (*nule třetin*? *nula třetinám*?). The Slovak rule (Peciar 1958, *Kultúra slova* 2002) is codified
  for Slovak only.

**Verdict.** **Closed as deliberately unsupported — attested variation, no canonical form**: a single-output API would
have to select a variant arbitrarily. This replaces the NTS-45 status "usage attested, not normative". The nominative
*nula lomeno třemi* is unchanged.
**Tests.** `E_CzechZeroOblique_DeliberatelyUnsupported` (0/3, 0/5, 0/100 in genitive, dative, accusative, locative,
instrumental; nominatives *nula lomeno třemi*, *nula lomeno pěti*, *nula lomeno stem*).

## C. SK — genitive singular of a generated *-ón* noun

**Before.** `ConvertFraction(10^12, 3, "case=genitív")` → `NotSupportedException`: the `-ón` `<GeneratedScaleForm>` had no
`singular.genitive` key (the ending is lexical in the pattern *dub*, *-a* or *-u*, and only *bilión -a* was known), so
`DeclineScale` found no form and the plugin failed closed.

**Evidence.**
- *bilióna*: **attested** — *bilión, -a, m.* (PSP 1940, p. 144, #1), *bilión -a m. čísl. zákl.* (KSSJ 4, #3), *bilión ‑a
  čísl. m.* (current PSP, #2).
- *trilióna*: **attested** — *trilión, -a, m.* (PSP 1940, p. 412, #1). The current PSP text has no entry *trilión* (#2).
- *kvadrilióna*: **attested** — *kvadrilión ‑a m.* (current PSP, #2). The PSP 1940 has *kvadrilión, -u, m.* (p. 237):
  the older form, superseded by the current codification.
- *kvintilióna*: **attested** — *kvintilión ‑a m.* (current PSP, #2).
- *milióna*: *milión, -a, m.* (PSP 1940, p. 254), the static model of the family.
- **Class rule**: "Číslovky milión, bilión, trilión majú substantívnu povahu a skloňujú sa podľa vzoru dub" (L. Navrátil,
  *Kultúra slova* 2003, p. 341, #4).
- **Higher Conway names**: productive continuation of the same series (the prefix invariant, the suffix fragment
  inflected), as AGENTS.md allows for generated names.
- A test comment of NTS-42 F attributed *trilión -u*, *kvadrilión -u* to the online Slovník cudzích slov: that dictionary
  gives definitions without grammatical marks (#5); the comment is corrected.

**Change.** `NumberConvertionConfiguration.SK.xml`, `<GeneratedScaleForm suffix="0">`:
`<Form key="singular.genitive" value="óna" />`, with its source comment. The engine joins the Conway prefix and the
fragment as for every other key (`bilión` → `bilióna`, `milikvadrilión` → `milikvadrilióna`); the plugin already read it
through `IScaleNounSource` / `ReadScaleNoun` / `DeclineScale`, so no C# changed (two XML doc comments updated).
