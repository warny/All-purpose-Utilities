### Mechanism

No engine, XSD or public-API change in the conversion pipeline. New public, additive types: the abstract
`OrdinalDomainGuardLanguageSpecifics` (a `<LanguageSpecifics>` plugin that rejects values outside a validated
domain and otherwise lets the declarative pipeline form the ordinal) and twelve sealed guards
(`Spanish…`, `Portuguese…`, `Galician…`, `Greek…`, `Finnish…`, `Russian…`, `Vietnamese…`, `Hindi…`,
`Japanese…`, `Korean…`, `Chinese…`, `BasqueOrdinalLanguageSpecifics`). Existing plugins: `ScandinavianOrdinalLanguageSpecifics`
gained the protected virtual `MaxSupportedOrdinal` and `Fuse` (Swedish overrides both), `PolishOrdinalLanguageSpecifics`
builds 1000–1999 and gained a `long` overload that fails closed, `HungarianOrdinalLanguageSpecifics` and
`UkrainianOrdinalLanguageSpecifics` changed their single-million wording. Configuration-only corrections for NL
(stem rules with suffix `e`, `Anywhere` replacements), ES, GL, EL, EU and the PT clock. ApiCompat against master:
no breaking change (non-strict); strict mode lists only the additions.

### Cardinal changes in scope

Only where a cardinal made an audited ordinal wrong: NL (one word up to *duizend*, diaeresis: 1 932 values of
0–2100 and the samples changed) and ES (`veintidós`, `veintitrés`, `veintiséis` and every number containing them,
65 values). Every other cardinal defect found on the way is recorded in NTS-27, not fixed.

### Before/after dump

All built-in cultures, cardinals 0–2100 and samples to `long.MaxValue`, ordinals 0–2100 and samples for every
variant combination, the clock every five minutes for each variant combination: the only changed cultures are
those listed above (ES, NL cardinals; SV, HU, UK, PL, RU, NL, ES, PT, GL, EL, FI, HI, JA, KO, ZH, VN, EU ordinals;
PT clock) and their aliases. No clock other than PT and no decimal, fraction or year output changed.

### Technical tests

- `NumberToStringOrdinalDomainGuardTests` (new): largest validated value and first rejected value of every guard,
  negative and above-`int` values, no unchanged cardinal returned on 0–2100, Finnish case restriction, zero policy.
- `NumberToStringOrdinalPluginTests`: Swedish expectations that pinned the contradicted forms corrected
  (`tjugoentusende`, fail-closed above a million); Hungarian `egymilliárdodik`; Greek feminine sweep now asserts the
  37 supported values and the fail-closed rest.
