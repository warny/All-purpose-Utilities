# NTS-30 and NTS-38 — Vietnamese zero classes, Spanish currency numerals, Spanish and Basque ordinals (2026-10-23)

NTS-30 is closed with its last two items (VN whole zero class, ES currency apocope); NTS-38 is closed. Starting
master: `340b08f0` (NTS-33/48/49, #655). Sources: `docs/NTS-08-linguistic-sources.md`, section NTS-30 and NTS-38.
Opened: NTS-50 (Basque *zerogarren*) and NTS-51 (currency phrases: ES *un millón de euros*, IT *un euro*).

## NTS-30 / VN — whole zero class

### Root cause

`ConvertRaw` only rendered a group when `group != 0`. A whole zero group therefore never reached `ConvertGroup`, the
inner forms, the triggers, the replacements nor the scale noun: no configuration could express *năm mươi triệu
không nghìn một trăm linh hai* (SGK Toán 4, Chân trời sáng tạo, Bài 25: model *307 000 262 Đọc số: Ba trăm linh
bảy triệu không nghìn hai trăm sáu mươi hai*; *Ba triệu không nghìn không trăm mười ba: 3 000 013*).

### Primitive — `internalZeroGroupString`

- XML attribute of `<Language>` (XSD documented); `NumberToStringConverterOptions.InternalZeroGroupString`; read back
  as `NumberToStringConverter.InternalZeroGroupString`; public XML model `LanguageType.InternalZeroGroupString`;
  copied by the options copy constructor; `baseOn`: absent inherits, explicit empty removes; white space = absent.
  A string: the snapshot is immutable by construction.
- Semantics: while `ConvertRaw` walks the groups from the units upward, a zero group is rendered when a lower group is
  non-zero (tracked) and a higher one is too (the loop condition). Leading and trailing zero groups are never
  rendered. Every internal zero group is rendered, consecutive ones included (no compaction: a language wanting it
  would need another primitive). Under `pairedScalesFrom` the zero slot is the whole paired multiplier.
- Rendering: the literal stands for the digits (no digit table, no multiplier variant rule), then the group triggers,
  the scale noun in the form the scale selects for a count of zero, the scale replacements, the scale-specific
  variant rules and the group-with-scale triggers, exactly like a non-zero group. Junctions: the zero group takes part
  in the inter-group assembly (value 0, so a `groupConnector` applies before it); the final connectors still precede
  the last non-zero group.
- Three Vietnamese phenomena stay distinct: the whole zero class *không nghìn* (this primitive), the zero hundreds
  *không trăm* and the zero tens *linh* of a non-zero class after a higher class (`innerBuildString`, NTS-27).

### VN configuration and guard

`internalZeroGroupString="không"`. `VietnameseOrdinalLanguageSpecifics` no longer closes a whole zero class; it keeps
closed two consecutive internal zero classes, which the cardinal reads class by class (the textbook method) but no
example attests.

## NTS-30 / ES — currency numerals

### Root cause

`ConvertCurrency` rendered the amount with the caller's query (+ the `CurrencyDefinition` forced variants). The
Spanish apocope (`uno → un`, `veintiuno → veintiún`) is configured under `gender=masculino` + `form=attributive`, and
only the time units forced `form=attributive`: `uno euro`, `veintiuno euros`. The apocope is a property of the
numeral governing a masculine noun (DPD "uno" §2.2), not of the word *euro*: it must apply to any caller-defined
currency, while the gender stays the currency's (the configuration cannot infer it from an arbitrary string).

### Primitive — `<Currency forceVariants>`

- XML element `<Currency forceVariants="…" />` in `<Language>` (XSD; public `CurrencyType`, `LanguageType.Currency`);
  `NumberToStringConverterOptions.CurrencyForcedVariants`; read back canonicalized as
  `NumberToStringConverter.CurrencyForcedVariants`; validated at construction like the time units (`UNTS006`,
  constituent `Currency`); copied by the options copy constructor; `baseOn`: an absent element inherits, a declared
  one replaces (`<Currency />` removes). No dimension name is hard-coded in the engine.
- Precedence, from the weakest: the caller's variants, the language's currency context, the `CurrencyDefinition`'s
  `UnitForcedVariants` / `SubunitForcedVariants` — the order of the time units (unit constraints over the caller's)
  with the currency, which knows its noun, as the most specific. A caller variant `form=standalone` therefore does
  not undo the context; a currency declaring `form=standalone` does. Applied to the unit and the subunit alike.

### ES configuration

`<Currency forceVariants="form=attributive" />`.

| 21 pesetas (`UnitForcedVariants` gender=femenino) | veintiuna pesetas | unchanged |

## NTS-38 — Basque ordinals

Euskaltzaindia rule 203 (2025) §5 supersedes rule 18: "Oro har, ordinalak eratzeko, -garren atzizkia atxikitzen zaio
zenbatzaile kardinalari", with *1 000 000. milioigarren*, *2 000 000. bi milioigarren*, *1 000 001.: milioi bat eta
batgarren*. Configuration: `<OrdinalScale scales="2" multiplierSeparator=" " />` (the round million is its noun,
after a multiplier other than one); guard widened from 999 999 999 to the cardinal domain, 999 999 999 999, by the
general rule on the cardinal *mila milioi* (rules 7, 203). Zero stays closed (NTS-50: the table lists *zerogarren*).

|---|---|---|
| 1 000 000 | milioi batgarren | milioigarren (attested) |
| 2 000 000 / 1 000 001 / 10 000 000 | bi milioigarren / milioi bat eta batgarren / hamar milioigarren | unchanged |
| 1 000 000 000 | `NotSupportedException` | mila milioigarren |
| 1 000 000 001 | `NotSupportedException` | mila milioi eta batgarren |
| 2 000 000 000 / 1 001 000 000 | `NotSupportedException` | bi mila milioigarren / mila eta bat milioigarren |
| 2 500 000 000 | `NotSupportedException` | bi mila eta bostehun milioigarren |

## NTS-38 — Spanish ordinals

DPD "ordinales" §2d: "se forman prefijando al ordinal simple el cardinal que lo multiplica, y posponiendo los
ordinales correspondientes a los órdenes inferiores: … milmillonésimo (1 000 000 000.º)"; DLE *milmillonésimo* "Que
ocupa en una serie el lugar número mil millones".

- Engine (generic): `TryBuildRoundScaleText` skipped nothing, so a round 10^9 under `pairedScalesFrom` met the unnamed
  upper index 3, which has no `OrdinalScale` rule, and fell back to the cardinal path. The unnamed upper half of a
  pair is now skipped: the value is read at the named scale below it, whose multiplier spans the pair (1000 × millón
  → `mil` + `millón` → `milmillonésimo`), as the cardinal does. Only ES and EU combine pairs and `OrdinalScale`; FI
  pairs without `OrdinalScale`.
- Configuration: the million composition widened to `1000001..999999999999` (lower orders after the head).
- Guard: domain 1–999 999 999 999; `mil` (1000) admitted as a soldered multiplier; every multiplier of *millón* from
  1001 is written in several words and stays closed, as do the multipliers ending in *uno* and the feminine hundreds
  (NTS-28). The obsolete "the cardinal is not composed" sentence is removed.

| Value | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| 1 000 000 000 | `NotSupportedException` | milmillonésimo (attested) |
| 1 000 000 001 | `NotSupportedException` | milmillonésimo primero |
| 1 000 000 001 (`gender=femenino`) | `NotSupportedException` | milmillonésima primera (DPD §3) |
| 1 000 002 000 | `NotSupportedException` | milmillonésimo dosmilésimo |
| 2 000 000 000 / 1 001 000 000 / 21 000 000 000 | `NotSupportedException` | unchanged (several-word multiplier) |
| 1 000 000 / 2 000 000 / 500 000 000 | millonésimo / dosmillonésimo / quinientosmillonésimo | unchanged |
