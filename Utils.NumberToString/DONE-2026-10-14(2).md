# NTS-30D — Bulgarian conjunction *и* before the last component (2026-10-14)

Split out of NTS-30, after NTS-30C. Starting master: `fffcc8305f00825eb7de617c48c7520062d60503`.
Sources: `docs/NTS-08-linguistic-sources.md`, section NTS-30D.

## Root cause

Bulgarian cardinals were composed with the digit `buildString` alone (`двадесет *`, `сто *`) and the plain group
separator, so no *и* was ever written: `двадесет едно`, `сто едно`, `хиляда едно`, `двадесет една хиляди`,
`двадесет един милиона`. NTS-30B and NTS-30C fixed the scale nouns and the multiplier gender only.

## Linguistic rule

Речник на българския език (ИБЕ-БАН), *и*, sense 3: in compound numerals *и* "свързва последното числително с
предходното". The choice is **local to each numeral phrase**, not global to the string: Sofia University (ezik.bg,
"Числително име") gives *един милиард двеста милиона шейсет и пет хиляди седемстотин осемдесет и пет*, i.e. one *и*
in the multiplier of *хиляди* and one in the last group. Hence:

- inside a group, *и* before the last non-zero component, once; 11–19, the round tens and hundreds are single
  numerals (*сто и петнадесет*, *сто и двадесет*, *сто двадесет и едно*);
- between groups, *и* before the last non-zero group when it is a single component (*хиляда и сто*, *две хиляди и
  пет*, *двадесет и една хиляди и едно*), never when that group has its own *и* (*хиляда сто двадесет и едно*);
- a multiplier keeps its own *и*, then its noun-governed gender, then the noun (*двадесет и една хиляди*).

## Existing primitives (why not)

- `groupConnector` inserts a word between the last two groups by the **value** of the lower group. It reaches
  1001 or 1100 with a threshold, but never the inside of a group (21, 121) nor a multiplier (21 000), and a value
  threshold cannot tell *хиляда и сто* (single component) from *хиляда сто и едно*.
- `intraGroupConnector` acts only at the hundreds junction under a threshold; 21 is out of its reach, and lowering
  the threshold to cover 120 while also configuring the tens would produce *сто и двадесет и едно*.
- `buildString` / `innerBuildString` are local to one position: the tens digit cannot know whether it is the last
  component of its phrase (*двадесет и едно* but not *двадесет и една хиляди сто…* at the hundreds).
- `Trigger` would mean textual patterns per tens, hundreds and scale word over rendered text; the rule is numeric.

## Generic primitive

- XML: `<Language finalComponentConnector="…">`; programmatic `NumberToStringConverterOptions.FinalComponentConnector`
  (copied by the options clone), read back as `NumberToStringConverter.FinalComponentConnector`. White space or
  absence means none. `baseOn`: absent inherits, an explicit value replaces, an explicit empty value removes it
  (same convention as `mixedFractionSeparator`).
- Composition metadata: the private `ConvertGroup(…, out bool hasFinalConnector)` reports whether the connector was
  placed inside the group. Walking outward from the last component, the first junction joining a **non-zero** digit
  to a non-empty lower part receives it; outer junctions see the flag and compose normally; a zero digit passes the
  flag through; an exception returns early as one component. `ConvertRaw` keeps the flag per group and, at the last
  junction of the whole number, inserts `separator + connector + separator` when the last non-zero group's
  multiplier has none. One boolean per group: no regex, no parsing of rendered text, no allocation; languages
  without the connector take the historical branches.
- The connector is raw composition: the multiplier replacements and variant rules (NTS-30B `една`/`един`/`два`), the
  forced variants and shielding of a generated scale (NTS-30C), the end triggers and `FinalizeWriting` (once) all run
  after it, unchanged.
- Rejected at construction with `groupConnector`, `intraGroupConnector` or any `Fusion` (default or scale-scoped
  table): they rewrite the same junctions and their joint semantics are undefined. `scaleConnector` is orthogonal.

## Ordinals, clock, other surfaces

- `BulgarianOrdinalLanguageSpecifics` already applied the same local model and does not call the cardinal engine:
  kept as is, byte-identical (*двадесет и първи*, *сто и първи*, *хиляда и първи*, *двадесет и една хиляди и
  първи*).
- Clock hours (1–12) are single components; the literal *и* of the patterns is not doubled.
- Decimals, fractions, `Number`, currency and years reuse the corrected cardinal fragments (*двадесет и едно цяло
  пет*, *двадесет и едно на сто и едно*, *двадесет и един лева и двадесет и една стотинки*); the decimal separator,
  the fraction connector and the caller's currency connector are unchanged. The case of the fraction denominator
  stays open (NTS-30).
