# Utils.NumberToString audit closures — 2026-10-03

NTS-10, NTS-11 and NTS-12 are closed, test-first: every language scenario and engine test was run
red before the configuration or engine change. Sources and decisions are recorded in
`docs/NTS-08-linguistic-sources.md` ("NTS-10, NTS-11 and NTS-12"). A before/after dump of every
built-in language (cardinals 0–1100 plus large samples, ordinals, every variant value, five-minute
clock times) confirmed that each commit only changed the language it targeted.

## New engine primitive — `<Fusion>`

`<Digit>` elements (Groups levels 2+) accept `<Fusion for="…" removeLeft removeRight left right/>`.
When a rule matches the numeric value of the lower sub-group, the digit text (left constituent) and
the sub-group text (right constituent) are concatenated directly after the rule's edge changes,
instead of going through `buildString`. Rules are cumulative from the widest to the narrowest range;
equally specific disagreeing rules, duplicate ranges, ranges outside the sub-group domain, empty
values, level-1 fusions, fusions overlapping the intra-group connector, fusions in scale prefix
tables, and `removeLeft`/`removeRight` edges absent from the actual constituents are rejected at
load. Rules are compiled at construction into immutable dense tables (no range parsing on the
conversion path, nothing at all for a language without fusions). They follow the `Groups` `baseOn`
rule. Engine tests: `NumberToStringFusionTests` (45).

`<Fusion>` is intra-group only. It is used by Italian; Hindi (lexicalized forms → `<Exceptions>`),
Arabic and Hebrew (scale-group junctions → `Replacement`) deliberately do not use it.

## NTS-10 — cardinal defects found during NTS-08

- **IT**: tens and hundreds digits declare fusions (`ventuno`, `ventotto`, `ventitré`, `centotré`,
  `centottanta`; `centouno`/`centootto` keep the vowel); the thousands are joined and accented by
  `onScale`/final Replacements (`duemila`, `milleuno`, `milletré`, `ventitremila`). Feminine
  compounds stay in `-uno`. The clock moves to a five-minute step. `diciannovesimo` is fixed, and
  `ItalianOrdinalLanguageSpecifics` makes compound ordinals and zero fail closed instead of
  producing `ventunoesimo` (follow-up: NTS-13).
- **HI**: 72 lexicalized exceptions for 21–99 (`इक्कीस`), reused inside larger numbers and ordinals.
- **AR**: the thousands take the attached `و` (`ألف وواحد`, `ألف ومائة`); the feminine variant now
  reaches a final unit written with that proclitic (`ألف وواحدة`, and likewise `مائة وواحدة`).

## NTS-11 — Hebrew cardinals from 1000

`אלף`, `אלפיים`, `שלושת` … `עשרת אלפים`, the masculine multiplier from 11000 (`אחד עשר אלף`), and
`ו` before the last element only (`אלף ואחד`, `אלף מאה עשרים ואחד`, `אלפיים ועשרים`). The
multiplier is set on the thousands group before variants, so a gender variant only reaches the lower
group. Ordinals from 1000 are the agreeing cardinal; the standalone ordinal variant also makes a
final `ועשר` masculine (`מאה ועשרה`). The meta-test exemption `ThousandsExemptions = ["HE"]` was
removed.

## NTS-12 — zero ordinal

`ApplyOrdinalTransform` reports explicitly whether a word rule or suffix was applied; for zero only,
the declarative pipeline throws `NotSupportedException` when no zero exception, word rule, suffix or
prefix formed the ordinal. Non-zero ordinals equal to their cardinal remain valid. Per language:
FI `nollas`; PL, ES, GL, PT, EL and HE fail closed (each pinned by a scenario). Engine tests:
`NumberToStringOrdinalZeroTests` (12); guard: `BuiltInLanguages_NeverReturnTheCardinalZeroAsOrdinal`.
