# Utils.NumberToString audit closure — 2026-10-11 (NTS-29, NTS-32)

## NTS-29 — attested ordinals of zero (closed)

NTS-08 recorded two attested ordinals of zero that still failed closed. Both were written as `.feature`
expectations and run red before the change. Sources: section "NTS-29 and NTS-32" of
`docs/NTS-08-linguistic-sources.md`.

| Lang. | Before | After | Source | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DA | `NotSupportedException` | *nulte* | Den Danske Ordbog *nulte* (ordenstal); Retskrivningsordbogen since 1986 (Dansk Sprognævn SV00001178) | New protected virtual `ScandinavianOrdinalLanguageSpecifics.ZeroOrdinal` (default `null`: zero declined, historical behaviour), overridden by `DanishOrdinalLanguageSpecifics` only |
| HU | `NotSupportedException` | *nulladik* | MTA "Számok" tool [AkH11-290, AkH12-292] | `HungarianOrdinalLanguageSpecifics`: `0 => "nulladik"`; negative values still declined |

Norwegian and Swedish keep failing closed for zero (pinned by a new "not supported" scenario in each feature
and by `NumberToStringNts29Nts32Tests`); Polish zero is unchanged (NTS-12). The cardinals *nul* and *nulla* are
unchanged. Zero has no variant in either language: every Danish gender variant gives *nulte*.

The technical tests that pinned DA and HU zero as unsupported (`ScandinavianOrdinals_OutsideImplementedRange_FailClosed`,
`HungarianOrdinals_OutsideImplementedRange_FailClosed`) were demonstrably wrong once the forms were sourced and were
updated: DA now expects *nulte*, NO/SV still expect the exception; the HU zero row was removed.

## NTS-32 — Polish ordinal of 400 (closed)

| Before | After | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| *czterosetny* (30 forms) | *czterechsetny* (30 forms) | Wielki słownik języka polskiego PAN, hasło 39657 (definition and full declension table); SJP PWN *czterechsetny* (KJP *czterechsetna rocznica*); SJP PWN has no entry *czterosetny*; PWN Poradnia 16446 (*osiem tysięcy czterechsetny*) |

The 400 row of the plugin's hundreds table now uses the stem *czterechsetn-* with the endings of the other
rows (identical to the WSJP table: m. sg. *czterechsetny/-ego/-emu/-y/-ym/-ym*, f. *-a/-ej/-ej/-ą/-ą/-ej*,
n. *-e/-ego/-emu/-e/-ym/-ym*, virile pl. *-i/-ych/-ym/-ych/-ymi/-ych*, other pl. *-e/-ych/-ym/-e/-ymi/-ych*).
The reference word rule `czterysta → czterosetny` of the XML (not reached: the plugin takes priority) was
aligned. Unchanged: 401 *czterysta pierwszy*, 415 *czterysta piętnasty*, 4000 *czterotysięczny*, 300
*trzechsetny*, 500–900, the NTS-28 thousands logic and the virile teens fix (59ee5229).

Dump of every converter (cardinals, ordinals in every variant combination, clock, decimals, fractions, years,
currencies, multiplicatives, time units) before (master a567115f) and after, line by line: **70 lines changed,
all expected**:

| Converter | Lines | Change |
|---|---|---|
| DA, DA-DK | 3 + 3 | ordinal 0 (default, `gender=fælleskøn`, `gender=intetkøn`): exception → *nulte* |
| HU, HU-HU | 1 + 1 | ordinal 0: exception → *nulladik* |
| PL | 62 | ordinals 400 and 1400 in the 31 variant combinations (default + 5 rodzaj × 6 przypadek): *czterosetn-* → *czterechsetn-*, endings identical |

No cardinal, clock, decimal, fraction, year, currency, multiplicative or time-unit output changed; NO, SV and
every other converter are byte-identical. (The dump samples 400 and 1400; 2400, 5400, 8400 and 4400 are pinned
by the feature and technical tests.)

## API

One protected virtual property added to the public abstract `ScandinavianOrdinalLanguageSpecifics`
(`ZeroOrdinal`); no generic engine primitive. ApiCompat against master (a567115f): non-strict passes; strict
reports only CP0002 for the added `ZeroOrdinal` getter. The package README notes the two zero ordinals.
