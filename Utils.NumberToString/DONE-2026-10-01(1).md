# Utils.NumberToString audit closures — 2026-10-01

## NTS-08 — implementation pass (subset closed, linguistic validation open)

Every remaining natural-language configuration received tested behaviour or an explicit deferral,
test-first: language scenarios were written and run red before each configuration or plugin was
added. Sources, decisions, and deviations from the initial working matrix are recorded in
`docs/NTS-08-linguistic-sources.md`. Only the configurations backed by consulted sources (NL, DA,
NO, BG, CS, SK, RO, TR, SW, ZU, EU, and after the review follow-up HR, AR, HI, KO, ZH) count as
linguistically verified; the other 15 remain open in `TODO.md` for a sourced review.

### Newly ordinal

DA, NO, SV (`ScandinavianOrdinalLanguageSpecifics` family), BG (`BulgarianOrdinalLanguageSpecifics`,
gendered), CS, SK, UK (`SlavicAdjectivalOrdinalLanguageSpecifics` family, gender × case), RO
(`RomanianOrdinalLanguageSpecifics`, DOOM), FA (declarative), TR (declarative, vowel harmony).

### Partial ordinals repaired

- HR: the `suffix="i"` fallback (`četirii`, `jedani`) is replaced by word rules for every final word;
  the scale nouns agree with their multiplier (`dvije tisuće`, `pet milijuna`, `dvije milijarde`),
  1000/1001 give `tisućiti`/`tisuću prvi`, and unverified round scale ordinals fail closed.
- HU: the `suffix="."` pseudo-ordinals are replaced by `HungarianOrdinalLanguageSpecifics`.
- AR: ordinals 20–99, 100, and 1000 (`ArabicOrdinalLanguageSpecifics`); other values fail closed.
- HE: above ten the ordinal is the agreeing cardinal (intended), masculine by default.
- VN: `thứ tư` instead of `thứ bốn`.
- AR/HE cardinals 11–99 and the hundreds connector were repaired because the ordinal policy relies
  on them; the two scenarios that pinned ungrammatical twelves were corrected.

### Newly ClockTime

NL, DA, NO, SV, BG, HR, HU, CS, SK, UK, PL, RU, ES, IT, PT, GL, RO, EL, FI, AR, HE, FA, TR, HI, JA,
KO, ZH, VN, SW (six-hour offset), ZU (literal class-5 hour forms), EU (literal clock-case forms).
All are neutral 12-hour clocks without day-part wording. No engine change was needed: every
convention uses `HourOffset`, `HourCycle`, `DisplayHourRange`, `HourForcedVariants`, and the
cardinal/ordinal/time-unit hour forms.

### Technical coverage

- `NumberToStringOrdinalPluginTests`: largest supported values, round scales, fail-closed ranges,
  declarative sweeps (HR, TR, FA), Greek feminine regression sweep.
- `NumberToStringClockTimeLanguageTests`: forced variants reaching the ordinal/cardinal pipeline,
  clock-only forms not leaking into `Convert`/`ConvertOrdinal`, exact `Convert(TimeOnly)` unchanged.
- `NumberToStringLanguageCoverageTests`: meta-coverage of capability scenarios, compound/hundred/
  thousand ordinals, and clock structural positions, by reflection over the generated scenarios.

### Still open

NTS-08 stays open for the linguistic validation of 15 reference-usage configurations and for its
deliberate deferrals (SW/ZU ordinals, EE/WO ClockTime). Defects discovered on the way are tracked as
NTS-10, NTS-11 and NTS-12.
