# Utils.NumberToString audit closure — 2026-10-14 (NTS-30B: low Slavic scales)

## NTS-30B — agreement of the scales 1–3 in Ukrainian, Bulgarian, Czech and Slovak

NTS-30 recorded (found by NTS-27): "UK, BG, CS, SK: the thousands and millions do not agree with their multiplier
(`два тисяч`, `две хиляда`, `dva tisíc`, `dva milion`)". The pass split that item out as NTS-30B, audited each
language against its own sources before changing anything, and fixed the scales 1–3 (thousand, million,
milliard). The generated Conway names from 10^12 were tested and deferred (NTS-30C, broadened); a Bulgarian
cardinal defect found on the way was opened as NTS-30D. Sources: `docs/NTS-08-linguistic-sources.md`, section
"NTS-30B".

### 1. Source audit

| Language | Rule established | Main sources |
|---|---|---|
| UK | *тисяча* feminine, *мільйон*/*мільярд* masculine, declined as nouns; nominative singular after …1 (not …11), nominative plural after …2–4 (not …12–14), genitive plural otherwise; *одна*/*дві* before *тисяча* | Український правопис 2019 § 70.11; ХНЕУ and Profpressa grammar notes (*три тисячі триста шістдесят чотири*, *п'ятсот п'ятдесят вісім тисяч гривень*); uk.wiktionary paradigms; CLDR uk |
| BG | *хиляда* → *хиляди* after any multiplier but one; *милион*/*милиард* take the count form *милиона*/*милиарда* after a numeral, including a compound ending in one; masculine *един*/*два* before them | Официален правописен речник, ИБЕ-БАН 2012, pp. 26–27 (*десет хиляди километра, един милион трактора, три милиона долара, пет милиарда лева, хиляда и една нощи*); ИБЕ-БАН "Езикови справки" (*двайсетте и един апарата*) |
| CS | nominative after exactly 1–4, genitive otherwise; after a compound ending in 1–4 the genitive is "more common and more natural" (*dvacet jedna žáků*, *dvacet dva žáků*), with the counting form kept; *miliarda* feminine | IJP (ÚJČ AV ČR) id 792 and 791 (*dva tisíce dvě stě třicet jedna*, *jeden milion tři sta tisíc sedm set osmdesát tři*), entries *tisíc*, *milion*, *miliarda*; Turinská (FF UK, 2009) (*dvacet jedna krav*, *dvacet jedna tisíc*); CLDR cs |
| SK | PSP: cardinals are one word, the thousands/hundreds/tens-with-units may be written apart for readability; tens with units always one word; hundreds and thousands one word with their multiplier (*tritisíc, dvadsaťsedemtisíc, stoosemdesiatdvatisíc*); *dvetisíc*; millions and milliards apart and agreeing (*štyri milióny, päť miliónov, tri miliardy*); a compound is indeclinable and governs the genitive plural (*dvadsaťdva žien*) | PSP 2000 (JÚĽŠ SAV) "Písanie slov osobitne a dovedna"; KSSJ 4 / PSP 2013 / OGS 2022 (*dvetisíc neskl.*, *miliarda -y -iárd ž.*); Jarošová, *Slovenská reč* 86/2 (2021), table 2.4.1; CLDR sk |

**Slovak decision: `dvetisíc`, not `dva tisíce`.** *dvetisíc* is the basic, indeclinable cardinal (PSP, KSSJ, OGS);
*dva tisíce, päť tisícov* is the substantive use of *tisíc*, declined, which Jarošová (2021, note 20) limits to a
numeral that does not determine a noun. A spelled-out cardinal is the basic form. The same PSP rule writes the tens
with units as one word, which the fused thousands require (*dvadsaťsedemtisíc*), so the Slovak tens and hundreds were
fused in the same pass (see section 5).

### 2. Architecture

| Language | Selector | `ScaleForm` | Multiplier gender | Other primitives |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| UK | new `UkrainianPluralLexicalFormSelector` (`one`/`few`/`many`) | 1 *тисяча/тисячі/тисяч*, 2 *мільйон/мільйони/мільйонів*, 3 *мільярд/мільярди/мільярдів* | `onScale="1"` `EndsWith`: *один тисяча → одна тисяча*, *два тисячі → дві тисячі* (as RU) | static names *тисяча*, *мільйон*, *мільярд* (the `(ів)` pseudo-plural removed from 2–3) |
| BG | `DefaultLexicalFormSelector` (`singular` = exactly 1, `plural` otherwise) | 1 *хиляда/хиляди*, 2 *милион/милиона*, 3 *милиард/милиарда* | `EndsWith`: *едно хиляди → една хиляди* (scale 1); *едно милион(а) → един милион(а)*, *две милиона → два милиона* and the same for *милиард* (scales 2–3) | — |
| CS | new `CzechSlovakPluralLexicalFormSelector` (`one`/`few`/`other`) | 1 *tisíc/tisíce/tisíc*, 2 *milion/miliony/milionů*, 3 *miliarda/miliardy/miliard* | `onValue`: *jedna milion → jeden milion* (scale 2, value 1), *dva miliardy → dvě miliardy* (scale 3, value 2) | — |
| SK | `CzechSlovakPluralLexicalFormSelector` | 2 *milión/milióny/miliónov*, 3 *miliarda/miliardy/miliárd* | `onValue`: *jeden miliarda → jedna miliarda*, *dva miliardy → dve miliardy* | default tables: tens `dvadsať*`, hundreds `päťsto *`; `<Groups onScale="1">` with hundreds `päťsto*`; `onScale="1"`: *dva tisíc → dvetisíc* (value 2), `EndsWith` *" tisíc" → "tisíc"* |

No language branch in the engine: everything is configuration over the existing `ScaleForm`, `onScale`/`onValue`
replacements and `<Groups onScale>` (NTS-20) primitives. The two new selectors only map an integer to a key.
`RussianPluralLexicalFormSelector` is unchanged in behaviour; its rule moved to the internal
`EastSlavicIntegerPluralRule`, shared with the Ukrainian selector (CLDR gives ru and uk the same integer rule; a
distinct public type keeps the Ukrainian configuration bound to its own language).

### 5. Slovak spelling

The PSP examples are reproduced exactly: *sedemtisíc dvesto osemdesiattri* (7 283), *pätnásť miliónov
tristoštyridsaťosemtisíc osemsto štyridsaťosem* (15 348 848), and Jarošová's *tisíc dvadsaťdva*, *tisíc sto dva*,
*tritisíc tristo dvadsaťdva*, *päťstoštyridsaťtritisíc tristo dvadsaťšesť*, *päť miliónov dvestotridsaťštyritisíc
päťsto šesťdesiatsedem*. The readable spaced variant is the one implemented (PSP also allows the whole number in one
word). A tens-with-units compound has no gender form (*dvadsaťjeden*, *dvadsaťdva* with `gender=ženský`, as
Jarošová's *dvadsaťdva žien*). `SlovakOrdinalLanguageSpecifics` already wrote *dvetisícdruhý*, *päťsto dvadsiaty
ôsmy*: the cardinal now follows the same rule.

### 7. Newly found, not fixed

- **NTS-30D** — Bulgarian compound cardinals omit *и* before their last component (`двадесет едно`, `сто едно`,
  `хиляда едно`), also inside a scale multiplier (`двадесет един милиона` for *двадесет и един милиона*). The *и*
  depends on the last component of the whole number, across groups; no current primitive expresses it.
- CS: the multiplier gender of 22–24 before *miliarda* in the genitive construction is not sourced (`dvacet dva
  miliard`).
- UK, BG, CS, SK `ConvertFraction`: the denominator is the plain nominative cardinal (`один на тисяча`); before this
  pass it was the invariant `тисяч`; neither was validated.
