# Utils.NumberToString audit closures — 2026-10-07 (4)

NTS-22 (Ewe million-scale cardinals) is closed. The Ewe domain is extended from 999 999 to
999 999 999 with the static scale name `miliɔn`; `biliɔn` and higher stay unsupported (opened
NTS-23). Configuration, tests and documentation only: no engine, API or XSD change. Sources and
decisions are recorded in `docs/NTS-08-linguistic-sources.md`, section "NTS-22".

Short or long scale: undecided. The two CLDR data sets disagree from 10^9 and no written Ewe source
reaches it, so nothing is claimed beyond the million.

## Variants

- `akpe akpe`: the Bible uses `akpe akpewo` only for an indefinite "thousands upon thousands" (GEN
  24:60, DAN 7:10, ECC 4:16, MIC 6:7) and `miliɔn` for exact millions. Documented, not produced; no
  `traditional|modern` variant is offered.
- `kpakple` before a small lower part after the millions (CLDR spellout only): not produced; tracked in
  NTS-23, where it would need a scale-dependent connector.

## Composition

- `miliɔn` + the standalone cardinal of the multiplier (`miliɔn eve`, `miliɔn blaeve vɔ ɖekɛ`,
  `miliɔn alafa eve`), then the lower groups as below a million: `kple` before a lower top group below
  100 (`miliɔn ɖeka kple ɖeka`, `miliɔn ɖeka kple akpe ɖeka`), a space otherwise (`miliɔn ɖeka akpe alafa
  ɖeka`). Empty groups are skipped. No `<Groups onScale>` table: the multiplier is the standalone cardinal.
- Ordinals: `-lia` on the last element (`miliɔn ɖekalia`, `miliɔn alafa evelia`, `miliɔn ɖeka akpe alafa
  ɖekalia`); never on `miliɔn`; no `<OrdinalScale>`.

## Domain and non-extrapolation

- `maxNumber="999999999"`; `NumberScale.CanNameGroup` is true for 0–2 and false from 3.
- `<Suffixes><Suffix>miliɔn</Suffix>` removed (it described no real system). No higher scale name is
  generated or configured (`biliɔn`, `triliɔn`, `quadriliɔn` …).

## Validation

- `Ewe.feature`: millions (attested: 1 000 000, 1 100 000, 200 000 000; the rest productive), the billion
  rejected, million ordinals. `NumberToStringNts22Tests`: scale contract and boundaries (cardinal
  `ArgumentOutOfRangeException`, ordinal `InvalidOperationException` at 10^9), the two corpus values, and
  sweeps over every multiplier 1–999 with 22 representative lower parts, cardinal and ordinal.
- Dump of the 73 built-in cultures: only EE lines differ from the post-NTS-20 dump.
