# NTS-50 and NTS-51 — Basque zero ordinal, Spanish and Italian currency phrases (2026-10-23)

NTS-50 and NTS-51 are closed. Starting master: `123b47ac` (NTS-30/38, #656). Sources:
`docs/NTS-08-linguistic-sources.md`, section NTS-50 and NTS-51. Opened: NTS-52 (the counted noun after a scale noun
in the other languages: Italian *un milione di euro*, …).

## NTS-50 — Basque ordinal of zero

### Source and cause

Euskaltzaindia rule 203 (2025) §5 lists `0. zerogarren` in its ordinal table, and its general rule forms the ordinal
from the cardinal ("-garren atzizkia atxikitzen zaio zenbatzaile kardinalari"). The configuration already formed it:
`BuildDeclarativeOrdinalFragment` renders zero with `Zero` (*zero*) and `ApplyOrdinalTransform` appends the suffix of
`<Ordinals suffix="garren">`. Only `BasqueOrdinalLanguageSpecifics` closed it, on the older rule 7, which gives the
cardinals *zero*/*huts* only.

### Change

The guard domain is 0–999 999 999 999 (was 1–). No `<OrdinalException value="0">`: the productive rule gives exactly
the normative form. No cardinal, fraction, decimal, clock, date or currency changed; no surface reads the ordinal of
zero (a denominator, an hour or a day is never 0).

| Value | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| EU, eu-ES ordinal 0 | `NotSupportedException` | zerogarren |
| EU cardinal 0 | zero | unchanged |
| EU ordinal 10^12 | `NotSupportedException` | unchanged |

## NTS-51 / ES — *de* after a numeral ending on *millón*

### Rule

DPD "cardinales" §6: *millón* and the higher scales are nouns; used alone, the counted noun follows *de* (*un millón
de personas*, *dos billones de pesos*); followed by other cardinals within a complex numeral, it does not (*un millón
doscientas mil personas*). The rule concerns the structure ending the numeral, not its value: *mil* (§6, a
determiner) never takes *de*.

### Primitive — `<Currency terminalScaleConnector terminalScales>`

- XML attributes of `<Currency>` (XSD documented); `NumberToStringConverterOptions.CurrencyTerminalScaleConnector` and
  `CurrencyTerminalScales` (range syntax); read back on the converter; copied by the options copy constructor;
  validated at construction (both or neither; non-blank connector; a parsable, non-empty range of scales ≥ 1).
- Structural helper `GetTerminalScaleIndex(BigInteger)` (internal): walks the groups exactly as `ConvertRaw` cuts
  them (`GroupPattern` widths, `pairedScalesFrom` pairs as one group at their named scale) and returns the scale of
  the lowest non-zero group, or `null` when that is the units group. The terminal scale is not the highest one:
  2 000 000 → 2, 2 500 000 → 1, 2 000 001 → null, 10^9 under ES pairing → 2 (the unnamed index 3 is never returned).
  Also `null`: zero, a whole-number exception (a literal, its structure unknown), a scale rendered without a noun,
  and a language placing the multiplier after the scale noun (the multiplier then ends the numeral). No text test.
- Applied per amount: the unit and the subunit are decided on their own counts (a 7-digit subunit can end on a
  scale).

### ES configuration

`<Currency forceVariants="form=attributive" terminalScaleConnector="de" terminalScales="2.." />`.

| Amount | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| 1 000 000 | un millón euros | un millón de euros |
| 2 000 000 | dos millones euros | dos millones de euros |
| 21 000 000 | veintiún millones euros | veintiún millones de euros |
| 10^9 | mil millones euros | mil millones de euros |
| 2 500 000 000 | dos mil quinientos millones euros | dos mil quinientos millones de euros |
| 1 000 000 pesetas (feminine) | un millón pesetas | un millón de pesetas |
| 1 200 000 / 1 000 001 / 2 500 000 001 / 1000 / 21 000 | — | unchanged (un millón doscientos mil euros, un millón un euros, …) |

The NTS-30 apocope and the precedence caller < `forceVariants` < `CurrencyDefinition` are unchanged; the connector is
independent of the variants.

## NTS-51 / IT — *un* before a masculine currency noun

### Rule

Treccani, "uno": the adjective/article *uno* has the apocopated masculine *un* "regolato dalle stesse norme che
regolano l'uso dell'art. determ. masch. sing. il, rispetto alla sua variante lo: un uomo, un osso, un cane, uno
scolaro, uno zoccolo". The onsets keeping *uno* are those of *lo* (Enciclopedia dell'Italiano, "articolo": z, x, i
semiconsonantica, s + consonante, sc, gn, ps; *pn* without rigour). Treccani, "uno, numerali composti con": the
compounds "tendono a rimanere invariati" (*ventuno banchi*, *un milione e uno cittadini*), the forms in *-un* being
admitted but less frequent. Decision: *uno* alone is apocopated, the compounds are left invariable.

### Primitive — `<Currency><Replacement exceptBeforeNoun>`

- `<Replacement>` children of `<Currency>`, of the new XSD type `CurrencyReplacementType` (oldValue, newValue
  required, scope, `exceptBeforeNoun`); `onScale`, `onValue` and `Variant` children are rejected by the schema (they
  have no meaning on a finished numeral) and `onScale`/`onValue` again by the converter for programmatic rules.
- Public `CurrencyReplacementRule` (a `ReplacementRule` + the noun beginnings, copied immutably), options
  `CurrencyReplacements`, converter `CurrencyReplacements` (immutable snapshot). The existing replacement engine
  (`ApplyVariantReplacement`, every `ReplacementScope`) applies them; no second engine.
- `exceptBeforeNoun`: comma-separated beginnings of the currency noun, compared ignoring case, before which the rule
  does not apply. Chosen with the user over an unconditional rule (which would read *un scellino*, *un zloty*,
  *un yen*) and over a caller-side opt-out.
- No `form` dimension was added to Italian: its ordinal configuration (exceptions, gender variants, `OrdinalScale`,
  `OrdinalComposition`) is untouched, and `SelectDefaultForm` sees the same dimensions as before.

### IT configuration

`<Currency><Replacement oldValue="uno" newValue="un" scope="Standalone"
exceptBeforeNoun="z,x,y,ia,ie,io,iu,gn,ps,sb,sc,sd,sf,sg,sh,sk,sl,sm,sn,sp,sq,sr,st,sv,sw,sz" /></Currency>`.

| Amount | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| 1 euro | uno euro | un euro |
| 1,01 euro | uno euro e uno centesimo | un euro e un centesimo |
| 1 scellino / zloty / yen | uno … | unchanged |
| 21 / 101 / 1001 / 1 000 001 euro | ventuno euro, … | unchanged (ventuno euro, un milione e uno euro) |
| 1 lira (gender=femminile) | una lira | unchanged |

## Currency pipeline

Each amount, unit then subunit: (1) the caller's variants, (2) overlaid by `<Currency forceVariants>`, (3) overlaid
by the `CurrencyDefinition` forced variants; (4) the cardinal fragment; (5) the currency replacements that apply
before the noun, in order; (6) the terminal scale; (7) the connector; (8) the noun. `uno → un` precedes *euro*; the
connector follows the whole numeral; the Spanish numeral is never seen by an Italian rule (per-language
configuration). `baseOn`: a `<Currency>` declared in a child replaces the parent's as a whole (attributes and
children), unchanged from #656, tested.
