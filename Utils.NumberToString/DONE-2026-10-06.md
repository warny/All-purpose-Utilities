# Utils.NumberToString audit closures — 2026-10-06

NTS-13 (Italian compound ordinals) is closed for its declared domain, test-first: the engine tests
and the Italian scenarios were run red before the engine and configuration changes. Sources and
decisions are recorded in `docs/NTS-08-linguistic-sources.md` ("IT compound ordinals (NTS-13)").
A before/after dump of every built-in language (cardinals and ordinals 0–2100 plus large samples,
every variant value, five-minute clock times) shows no difference after the engine commit, and
only Italian ordinal changes after the configuration commit — every changed value previously threw
`NotSupportedException`; the forms already produced (1–20, round tens, 100, 1000) are unchanged.

## New engine primitive — `<OrdinalStem>`

`<Ordinals>` accepts `<OrdinalStem from="…" to="…"/>` (public model `OrdinalStemRule`,
`NumberToStringConverterOptions.OrdinalStemRules`, converter `OrdinalStemRules`). On the suffixed
ordinal path, the ending of the last word is rewritten by the matching rule with the longest
`from` (ordinal, case-sensitive `EndsWith`; declaration order irrelevant), then the effective suffix
(variant override or base) is appended.

- Precedence is unchanged above it: ordinal exceptions, then exact `<Ordinal from to>` word rules.
- `removeTrailing` keeps its meaning and applies only when no stem rule matches; a configuration
  without stem rules produces byte-identical output.
- `from` is required and non-empty, `to` is required and may be empty (XSD and constructor); two
  rules with the same `from` are rejected at load, including inside a `baseOn` child.
- Rules are validated and snapshotted once at construction into an immutable array sorted longest
  first; nothing is sorted on the conversion path. The options copy constructor carries them.
- `baseOn` merges them by `from` (child replaces, new rules appended).
- Stem rules are declared on `<Ordinals>` only; variants reuse them with their own suffix.
  Variant-specific stem rules were not needed and are not part of the contract.

Engine tests: `NumberToStringOrdinalStemTests` (32), plus the snapshot immutability test in
`UtilsTest.Security`.

## NTS-13 — Italian compound ordinals

- `NumberConvertionConfiguration.IT.xml` replaces `removeTrailing="i"` and the 13 whole-word rules
  of each gender by eight stem rules: `centouno → centun`, `mila → mill`, `tré → tre`,
  `sei → sei`, and the final vowels `a`, `e`, `i`, `o` removed. The feminine variant keeps only its
  exceptions and `suffix="esima"`.
- Productive domain: 1–1999 and the round thousands 2000–999000, both genders: `ventunesimo`,
  `ventitreesimo`, `ventiseiesimo`, `ventiquattresimo`, `centunesimo`, `centotreesimo`,
  `centoseiesimo`, `centottantesimo`, `duecentesimo`, `milleunesimo`, `duemillesimo`,
  `diecimillesimo`, `centomillesimo`; `-21 → meno ventunesimo` (sign policy unchanged).
- `ItalianOrdinalLanguageSpecifics` builds nothing and reconstructs no cardinal: it only rejects
  zero (NTS-12 decision unchanged — no mechanical `zeresimo`), non-round thousands above 1999
  (opened as NTS-15) and one million and above (NTS-14, cardinals known wrong).
- Tests: the `Italian.feature` scenarios (sourced examples, feminine subset, rejected domain) and
  `NumberToStringItalianOrdinalSweepTests` (11–1999 and every round thousand in both genders, the
  2001–9999 non-round sweep, the fail-closed samples). The former "Unverified compound ordinals are
  rejected" scenario pinned the temporary NTS-13 limitation and was replaced by the reduced
  rejection domain.
