# NTS-40 and NTS-41 — oblique UK/CS/SK fractions and the large CS/SK slash readings (2026-10-16)

Opened by NTS-39. Starting master: `70f2ad4c` (NTS-39, #645). Implemented together because both tickets need declined
compound cardinals: NTS-41 for the *lomeno*/*lomené* denominators, NTS-40 for the numerators of the oblique cases.
Sources: `docs/NTS-08-linguistic-sources.md`, section NTS-40/41 (each form marked attested, rule or inference).

## Architecture

One **declined-cardinal builder per language**, private to its fraction plugin, used by both tickets:

| file | builder | used by |
|---|---|---|
| `UkrainianOrdinalLanguageSpecifics.FractionCases.cs` | `DeclineCardinal` (every part, §105) | NTS-40 numerators |
| `CzechOrdinalLanguageSpecifics.FractionCases.cs` | `DeclineCardinal` (every part, *sto* before a numeral undeclined) | NTS-40 numerators, NTS-41 *lomeno* denominators |
| `SlovakOrdinalLanguageSpecifics.FractionCases.cs` | `DeclineLastComponent` (last component, scales included) | NTS-40 numerators, NTS-41 *lomené* denominators |

The three classes became `partial`; the shared base keeps only the case plumbing (`FractionCase`,
`RequestedFractionCase`, the per-language `FractionCaseOrder`). Each language owns its morphology; there is no generic
Slavic declension framework, and BG is untouched.

The public `IFractionLanguageSpecifics` contract could not carry a declined numerator: the converter always renders
the numerator through the cardinal pipeline, which has no case forms. Rather than a public API, the plugins (in the
same assembly) use an **`internal`** factory, `FractionDenominatorReading.WithNumerator(numeratorText, text)`, and
`NumberToStringConverter.BuildFractionText` writes that text when present (three lines). Changed or not:

| surface | changed |
|---|---|
| `NumberToStringConverter` | yes, internal only: `BuildFractionText` honours `NumeratorText` |
| `IFractionLanguageSpecifics` | no (interface unchanged; `FractionDenominatorReading` gains internal members, remarks updated) |
| XSD | no |
| XML configurations | comments only |
| public API | no (ApiCompat normal and strict clean) |
| `Convert(…, "case=…")` | no: a cardinal keeps no case |

Answers to the NTS-41 questions: a final *milión* and a final *miliarda* are declinable (Morfológia: *milión*
"tendencia k neohybnosti je menšia", *miliarda* "nemá tendenciu k neohybnosti"); a scale noun followed by a lower
component keeps its absolute form (the last-component rule continues through the scales: *dva milióny troma*);
*číslom* stays necessary for an indeclinable last component (*sto*, *tisíc*, *-jeden*) and a bare final *jeden*.
Still closed: 10^12 and above.

## NTS-40 — Czech

- **Numerator declension**: every part (the NTS-41 builder); 1–4 agree with the noun, 5–99 impose the case on it (IJP),
  *sto* before a numeral undeclined.
- **Fraction noun declension**: the IJP paradigms (*třetině, třetinou, třetinám, třetinách, třetinami*); singular
  after one, plural in the case otherwise, the genitive plural after a numerator ending in *sto*/*tisíc*/*milion*/
  *miliarda* (IJP basic construction) and in the accusative after 5+.
- **Slash reading under a caller case**: not declined; an oblique case of a fraction without a noun fails closed (no
  source declines the *lomeno* phrase, whose denominator is already an instrumental).

Examples: *jedné třetiny*, *dvěma třetinám*, *třemi pětinami*, *dvě třetiny* (ACC), *dvaceti dvěma setinám*, *třemi
sty šedesáti pěti tisícinami*, *jednom milionu třech stech padesáti osmi tisících čtyřech stech dvaceti třech
miliontinách*, *dvěma tisícům třetin*. Still closed: 21/100, 101/3, 1001/3 outside the accusative; 21 000/3 outside
the accusative; zero; every *lomeno* fraction in an oblique case.

## NTS-40 — Slovak

- **Numerator declension**: the last component (the NTS-41 builder); 1–4 after their paradigms; *-jeden* compounds,
  *sto*, *tisíc* compounds absolute with the noun in the case; *milión*/*miliarda* declined with the noun in the
  genitive plural.
- **Fraction noun declension**: patterns *žena* (*tretine, tretinou, tretinám, tretinách, tretinami*) and *ulica*
  (*polovici, polovicou, poloviciam, poloviciach*).
- **Slash reading under a caller case**: as Czech, nominative only.

Examples: *jednej tretiny*, *dvom tretinám*, *troma pätinami*, *dvadsaťjeden stotinám*, *dvadsiatim dvom stotinám*,
*sto dvadsiatim sedminám*, *tristo šesťdesiatimi piatimi tisícinami*, *tisíc štyridsiatimi dvoma tretinami*, *dvoma
miliónmi tretín*, *jednu miliardu tretín* (ACC). Still closed: 101/3 and 1 000 001/3 outside the accusative; zero;
every *lomené* fraction in an oblique case.

## Review follow-up (PR #646)

The numerator a plugin spells for an oblique case (`FractionDenominatorReading.NumeratorText`) bypassed the caller's
`AdjustFunction`, which the cardinal pipeline applies to every numerator fragment (`BuildCardinalFragment` →
`ApplyRawAdjustment`): with an uppercasing adjustment, `ConvertFraction(5, 3)` gave `П'ЯТЬ третіх` but
`ConvertFraction(5, 3, "case=давальний")` gave `п'яти третім`. `BuildFractionText` now passes the spelled numerator
through `ApplyRawAdjustment` only (the text is already declined: the morphology rules and end triggers are not re-run).
Test `ObliqueNumerator_GoesThroughTheAdjustFunction` (UK, CS, SK): the adjusted numerator in the nominative and the
oblique case, and a single language finalization on the whole phrase. NumberToString tests: 6 782 passed; dump
unchanged from the first version (only UK, CS, SK fraction probes, all `NotSupportedException` → reading; BG and every
other surface byte-identical); ApiCompat normal and strict clean.
