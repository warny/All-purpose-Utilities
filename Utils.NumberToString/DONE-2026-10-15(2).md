# NTS-30G — Basque final *eta* and the Spanish masculine million multiplier (2026-10-15)

The two NTS-30 items opened by NTS-30F, closed in one PR as two independent generic slices. Starting master:
`5e5cc6dd` (NTS-30F, #642). Sources: `docs/NTS-08-linguistic-sources.md`, section NTS-30G.

## Slice 1 — `finalGroupConnector` (EU)

### Root cause

EU declared `groupConnector="eta" groupConnectorThreshold="1000"`: since every group multiplier is below 1000, an
*eta* was written at **every** junction between groups, then removed by the `eta X-ehun eta → X-ehun eta`
replacements when the following hundreds carried their own. Euskaltzaindia rule 203 §4 writes one *eta*, before the
last group only: *hirurogei milioi berrehun mila eta bat* (60 200 001), *bi milioi eta zazpiehun mila* (2 700 000).

`finalComponentConnector` (NTS-30D) could not be reused: it also joins the components inside a group, where Basque
already writes its *eta* in the `buildString`s (`ehun eta *`, `hamar eta *`); both mechanisms would claim the same
junctions.

### Engine

New generic `finalGroupConnector` (XML attribute on `<Language>`, XSD, `NumberToStringConverterOptions.FinalGroupConnector`,
read-only `NumberToStringConverter.FinalGroupConnector`, `baseOn` inheritance — absent inherits, explicit empty
removes —, copied by `new NumberToStringConverterOptions(converter)`).

- Semantics: while assembling the non-zero groups from the most significant down, the connector is written at the
  junction preceding the **last remaining** group (`groupsValues.Count == 1` in `ConvertRaw`), whatever that group
  holds. It never takes part in `ConvertGroup`.
- A paired multiplier (`pairedScalesFrom`) is rendered by its own `ConvertRaw`, so it places its own connector
  before its own last group: structural, no text parsing.
- Precedence at a junction: `finalComponentConnector` → `finalGroupConnector` → `groupConnector` (below its
  threshold) → `groupSeparator`.
- Combinations: with `finalComponentConnector` **rejected** at construction (`ArgumentException`, both claim the
  junction before the last group); with `groupConnector` allowed (the final junction takes `finalGroupConnector`,
  the others keep `groupConnector`); with `intraGroupConnector`, a `Fusion` or `scaleConnector` orthogonal (they act
  inside a group or between a multiplier and its noun).

### EU configuration

`groupConnector="eta" groupConnectorThreshold="1000"` → `finalGroupConnector="eta"`. The `eta X-ehun eta`
replacements are kept: they implement the rule-203 clause "dropped when the following hundreds carry their own".
Removed: the historical `bat ehun → ehun` (`scope="Anywhere"`) replacement. No group spells its hundreds *bat ehun*,
so it could only meet the junction of *milioi bat* with a following *ehun …* group, where it deleted the multiplier
(`milioi ehun mila eta bat`); the old every-junction *eta* had been masking it.

## Slice 2 — exact `<ScaleForm>` forced variants (ES)

### Root cause

ES declares `gender=masculino,femenino`; under `gender=femenino` the whole-number variant pass rewrote every
`-cientos` to `-cientas`, including the multiplier of *millones*: `quinientas millones`, `dos mil quinientas
millones`. *millón* is a masculine noun (DLE) and the hundreds agree with the noun they quantify (DPD "cardinales"
§3); the counted noun follows *de* (§6), so the multiplier of *millón* is masculine while the lower groups keep the
counted noun's gender (*un millón doscientas mil personas*). Derived from these rules, not printed verbatim.

### Engine

Exact scale forms may now force variants on the multiplier they govern, with the NTS-30C contract of
`GeneratedScaleForm`:

- XML: `forceVariants` on a `<ScaleForm><Forms><Form>` (XSD restricted to scale forms). Public XML model:
  `LexicalFormEntry.ForceVariants` (the class is shared with time-unit forms; the schema rejects the attribute there
  and the loader rejects it on a time unit built without the schema — decision taken with the maintainer, the only
  additive option since `XmlSerializer` ignores overridden or hiding members).
- Programmatic: `NumberToStringConverterOptions.ScaleFormMultiplierForcedVariants`
  (`IReadOnlyDictionary<int, IReadOnlyDictionary<string, ForcedVariantSet>>`: scale index → form key → forced set),
  parallel to `ScaleForms` / `ScaleFormSelectors` / `ScaleFormMultiplierSeparators`; read back canonicalized and
  immutable as `NumberToStringConverter.ScaleFormMultiplierForcedVariants`, copied by the options copy constructor.
  The same term as `GeneratedScaleForm.MultiplierForcedVariants`.
- Validation at construction: a null entry, or a key that names no effective form of its scale (the synthesized
  `singular`/`plural` included), is an `ArgumentException`; unknown dimensions or values are `UNTS006` with the path
  `ScaleForms[2].plural`; a scale listed only here is registered with its synthesized forms. The shared key/null
  validation is `GeneratedScaleForm.SnapshotMultiplierForcedVariants`, the shared canonicalization
  `CanonicalizeMultiplierForcedVariants`.
- `ScaleFormDefinition` carries `MultiplierForcedVariants`; `ResolveScaleWord` returns the selected exact form's
  forced variants (it returned `ForcedVariantSet.Empty`). `ConvertRaw` is unchanged: NTS-37 already resolves the
  word before rendering the multiplier, overlays the forced values, renders the whole (paired) multiplier and the
  group pipeline with them, and shields the group.
- Shielding: `NewShieldedGroups()` allocated a list only when a generated scale form existed, so an exact forcing
  form alone was never shielded and the caller's feminine pass rewrote it. It now allocates when any scale form can
  force variants (`_scaleFormsMayForceVariants`: any generated form, as before, or an exact form with forced
  variants); languages using neither still allocate nothing.
- `baseOn`: a child `<ScaleForm>` replaces the whole inherited entry of its scale, forced variants included; an
  absent entry is inherited unchanged.

`forceVariants="gender=masculino"`, default selector. Only the gender is forced: the `form` dimension (attributive
apocope) stays the caller's.

## Regression dump

All 74 converters against `5e5cc6dd` (dump extended with an NTS-30G section: the rule-203 values, million
multipliers and lower groups under every variant combination, negatives, decimals, currency, fractions, `Number`,
ordinals), 1 767 830 lines: **152 changed, in ES (58), EU (47) and eu-ES (47) only**.

- ES: every changed line has `gender=femenino` and differs only by `-cientas → -cientos` on words before
  *millones* (checked word by word): 40 cardinals, 4 negatives, 4 decimals, 4 currency amounts, 4 fractions, 2
  variant probes of NTS-30F. No masculine/default line, no standalone feminine number without a million, no ordinal
  and no clock line changed. The currency noun (`uno euro`, a separate TODO item) is untouched.
- EU/eu-ES: every changed line is the deletion of one or more *eta* tokens (checked word by word), except
  1 100 001, which also regains the *bat* the removed `bat ehun` rule deleted: 30 million-multiplier probes, 4
  rule-203 probes, 4 ordinals (the cardinal base of `ConvertOrdinal`, e.g. 2 200 022), 2 negatives, 2 decimals, 2
  fractions, 2 `Number`, 1 currency.
- Other exact-ScaleForm languages (RU, UK, BG, CS, SK, FI, AR, PL, …): byte-identical.
