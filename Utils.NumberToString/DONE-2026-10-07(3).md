# Utils.NumberToString audit closures — 2026-10-07 (3)

NTS-20 (Ewe cardinal system rebuild) is closed. The Ewe cardinals 0–999 999 are rebuilt on one
sourced orthography, the `-lia` ordinals withdrawn by NTS-16 are restored, and two generic engine
primitives were added on the way: `<Groups onScale>` (how a scale multiplier is built) and
`multiplierPosition` (how it is assembled with the scale noun). The Ewe configuration uses only the
second one. The million stays outside the domain (opened NTS-22). Sources and the full decision
record are in `docs/NTS-08-linguistic-sources.md`, section "NTS-20".

## Decisions

- **The multiplier of `akpe` is the standalone cardinal** (Bible: 41 000 `akpe blaene-vɔ-ɖekɛ`,
  144 000 `akpe alafa ɖeka blaene-vɔ-ene`, 601 000 `akpe alafa ade kple ɖeka`). Ewe therefore needs
  only `multiplierPosition="afterScale"`; no `<Groups onScale>` table duplicates the default one.
- **`kple` after the hundreds before 1–10 only** (`intraGroupConnectorThreshold="11"`): obligatory
  there in every source (Bible units 8/8, ten 4/4; Peace Corps and Omniglot 101). Before 11–99 it is
  optional (Bible teens 8/11, round tens 35/82, tens + units 77/106; Peace Corps 122 without), and the
  form without it is the only one in the attested ordinals 150th and 480th. The NTS-20 working
  hypothesis "110 → alafa ɖeka ewo" was contradicted by the Bible (4/4 `kple ewo`).
- **`kple` after the thousands before a lower part below 100** (`groupConnectorThreshold="100"`):
  Bible 8/8 below 100, 2/140 with hundreds.
- **`ɖekɛ` after `vɔ`**: eight whole-number exceptions (21, 31 … 91), like the lexicalized teens;
  the multipliers reuse them through the shared exceptions (41 000).
- **Zero `naneke o`**: the only answer of every zero row consulted; no source has a distinct numeral.
- **Ordinals**: `<Ordinals suffix="lia">` with `gbãtɔ` for 1; `ZeroOrdinalUnsupportedLanguageSpecifics`
  rejects zero. The suffix lands on the multiplier after `akpe` (`akpe evelia`), never on the noun.
  `<OrdinalScale>` is not used: Ewe does not form the ordinal of the scale noun.
- Not validated by NTS-20 and unchanged: `decimalSeparator="kpɔ"`, `fractionSeparator="kple"`,
  `minus="minus *"`; their output changes only lexically with the new cardinals.

## Engine primitives

- **`<Groups onScale="…">` / `NumberToStringConverterOptions.ScaleScopedGroups`**: digit tables
  rendering the multiplier of the covered scales; the default `<Groups>` stays the ordinary cardinal
  and the public `Groups` property keeps its type. A table replaces all levels at once, owns its
  fusions, and shares the whole-number exceptions. Ranges use the `Replacement onScale` syntax and are
  compiled once into a 64-entry scale-index lookup (ranges are consulted only beyond it). Rejected at
  construction: a null entry, a missing/empty range, an index below 1, a level set different from the
  default one, duplicate or overlapping ranges, invalid digit tables or fusions (diagnostics name
  `ScaleScopedGroups[i] onScale="…"`). XML: several `<Groups>` elements, one default without `onScale`
  (a second one is rejected); `baseOn` replaces an identical range, adds a disjoint one and rejects any
  other overlap.
- **`multiplierPosition="beforeScale|afterScale"` / `ScaleMultiplierPosition`**: orders the multiplier
  and the scale noun (`scaleConnector` stays between them). Default `beforeScale`.
- Replacements, variant rules, fusions and triggers keep their contexts: the scoped table is chosen
  before `ConvertGroup`, then the scale noun, the `onScale` replacements (`onValue` on the numeric
  multiplier), the scale variant rules and the triggers run as before.
- `<OrdinalScale>` keeps its semantics; it is rejected with `afterScale` and on a scale covered by a
  scoped table, since it assembles "standalone multiplier + noun" itself.
- Snapshots: conversions read private deep copies of every digit table; the exposed `Groups` and
  `ScaleScopedGroups` are further copies, and the options clone deep-copies both.

## Validation

- Before/after dump of the 73 built-in cultures (cardinals 0–2100 and large values, every variant,
  ordinals, clock): byte-for-byte identical after the engine change; after the configuration change
  only EE lines differ.
- `Ewe.feature` (each value tagged attested or productive), engine tests
  (`NumberToStringScaleScopedGroupsTests`: default unchanged, selection, ranges, open range beyond the
  lookup, validation, replacements, variant rules, fusions, triggers, position, ordinals, OrdinalScale,
  clone, XML, `baseOn`), mechanical sweeps (`NumberToStringNts20Tests`: every cardinal 0–999 999
  recomposed from the rules, every ordinal 1–999 999 = cardinal + `lia`), immutability
  (`UtilsTest.Security`).

## Remaining limits

- NTS-22: the million (`akpe akpe` vs `miliɔn ɖeka`; Bible `miliɔn ɖeka akpe alafa ɖeka`).
- No ordinal of zero; no Ewe clock-time (unchanged).
