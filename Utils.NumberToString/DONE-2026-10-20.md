# NTS-36 and NTS-42 F — Czech and Slovak generated scales (2026-10-20)

Starting master: `b4a3e189` (NTS-44, #649). Two tickets in one change because the second needs the first: NTS-36 fixes
the generated names themselves, NTS-42 F declines those same names in the fractions without a second Conway
nomenclature in the plugins. Scope: CS and SK only. No public API, no XSD and no Conway-engine change; no other language
configuration touched. Sources: `docs/NTS-08-linguistic-sources.md`, section "NTS-36 and NTS-42 F" (#1–#8 below refer
to it).

## NTS-36 — Czech and Slovak spelling of the Conway names

### Defect

CS and SK inherited the four Latin tables of `SCALE-LONG` (`Scale0Prefixes`, `UnitsPrefixes`, `TensPrefixes`,
`HundredsPrefixes`), so from 10^24 they wrote `quadrilion` / `quadrilión`, `quintiliarda`, `octilion`,
`quattuordecilion`, `quinquagintilion`, `octingentilion` — Latin spellings neither language uses: the IJP writes
*kvadrilion*, *kvintilion* (#1), the Slovak series is *kvadrilión*, *kvadriliarda*, *kvintilión* (#6).

The Conway construction, its linking markers (`tre(s)`, `se(xs)`, `(mxs)…`), the junction `li`, the suffix families
and their `forceVariants` (NTS-30C) are unchanged; the agreement is untouched (*dva kvadriliony*, *pět kvadriliard*,
*päť kvadriliárd*). Weakest point: *kvatuor* (no Czech or Slovak form of *quattuor* found; productive deduction from
the domesticated-loan rule, recorded as such).

### Tables

Each language overrides the four tables in its own `<NumberScale>` (`NumberConvertionConfiguration.CS.xml`,
`.SK.xml`). Every table differs from `SCALE-LONG` and `baseOn` overrides whole tables, so no finer override exists; CS
and SK keep one copy each, separately sourced, rather than a new shared base.

## NTS-42 F — Czech and Slovak fractions from 10^12

### Architectural cause

The fraction plugins (`CzechOrdinalLanguageSpecifics`, `SlovakOrdinalLanguageSpecifics`, NTS-40/41) decline every
cardinal themselves, from their own tables of *tisíc*, *milion*/*milión*, *miliarda*; they stopped at
`MaxDeclinedNumber = 999_999_999_999` because the names from 10^12 are generated by the converter's Conway machinery
(`NumberScale` + `<GeneratedScaleForm>`, nominative count forms only), which `IFractionLanguageSpecifics` does not
expose. Opening them in the plugins would have meant a second spelling of the Conway names.

### New abstraction

- **Data**: the declined forms are configuration. A `<GeneratedScaleForm>` family already holds suffix fragments by key
  (`one`, `few`, `other`); the CS and SK families now also declare twelve case keys, `singular.<case>` and
  `plural.<case>` (`nominative`, `genitive`, `dative`, `accusative`, `locative`, `instrumental`). The XSD key is free
  text and the selector never returns these keys, so cardinals and validation are unchanged.
- **Engine** (`NumberToStringConverter.ScaleForms.cs`): `TryGetScaleNounForm(scale, key)` resolves a form exactly as
  `ResolveScaleWord` resolves a count form (exact `<ScaleForm>`, else the `<GeneratedScaleForm>` of the suffix, whose
  fragment goes through the same `NumberScale.BuildDynamicName(prefix, fragment)`), without a selector. It is exposed
  through the internal interface `IScaleNounSource` (`IScaleNounSource.cs`), implemented by a private nested adapter
  created once per converter.
- **Plugin contract**: the internal `IScaleNounFractionLanguageSpecifics : IFractionLanguageSpecifics` adds an overload
  receiving the source; `BuildFractionText` calls it when the language implements it. The public
  `TryGetFractionDenominator` is unchanged and, called without the converter, keeps failing closed from 10^12.
- **Plugins**: the shared `ReadScaleNoun` (`SlavicAdjectivalOrdinalLanguageSpecifics`) reads the twelve keys of a scale
  and its gender (feminine when its `one` form forces the language's feminine on the multiplier, as the configuration
  declares: *jedna biliarda*). CS `DeclineCardinal` and SK `DeclineLastComponent` / `Cardinal` now walk every group of
  three digits (`BigInteger`): scales 1–3 keep their tables, scales from 4 read the converter. A key the configuration
  does not declare is a form without source: the reading fails closed.

**No Conway duplication**: the plugins hold no prefix, no table, no scale name above *miliarda* and never parse an
output; the noun is built by the same `BuildDynamicName` call as the cardinal, so a respelling (NTS-36) reaches both.
The 10^6024 tests decline *milikvadrilionu* / *milikvadriliónu*, names no plugin could know.

### Czech rules

Unchanged NTS-40 strategy: every part declined; the generated noun singular after one (*jednoho bilionu*, *jedné
biliardy*, *jedním bilionem*), the nominative plural after 2–4 and the genitive plural after the others in the direct
cases (*dva biliony*, *pět bilionů*), the plural of the case otherwise (*dvěma bilionům*, *třech bilionech*, *pěti
biliardami*); a counted noun in the genitive plural after a final scale noun (*jednomu bilionu třetin*), in the case
after a final non-scale group (*jednomu bilionu dvěma třetinám*); *lomeno* + the instrumental (*jedna lomeno jedním
bilionem jednou*). Paradigms: IJP (#1, #2).

### Slovak rules

Unchanged NTS-40/41/44 strategy: only the last component declined; a final generated noun with its multiplier like
*milión*/*miliarda* (*jednému biliónu*, *dvoma biliónmi*, *dvadsiatimi dvoma biliónmi*, an indeclinable multiplier
kept: *dvadsaťjeden biliónom*, *sto jeden biliónom*), the counted noun then in the genitive plural (Morfológia 330);
the words before the last component in their absolute form (*jeden bilión piatim tretinám*, *jeden bilión jeden
tretinám*); *lomené* + the instrumental or *lomené číslom* (*jedna lomené jedným biliónom*, *jedna lomené číslom jeden
bilión jeden*). Paradigms: *dub* (*-ón*) and *žena* (*-arda*) (#5, #8).

### Domain now supported

CS and SK oblique numerators with a fraction noun, and *lomeno*/*lomené* denominators, over the whole cardinal domain
(unbounded), under the same closures as below 10^12.

### Still closed (fail-closed, documented)

- SK genitive singular of a generated *-ón* noun (*jedného bilióna tretín*, *jedného trilióna …*): lexical ending in
  *dub*, attested for *bilión -a* only (#5, #8); no key declared.
- CS NTS-42 A (compound ending in *jedna*: 10^12 + 1 in an oblique case) and B (multiplier ending in one after the tens
  or hundreds: 21 × 10^12, 101 × 10^12 in an oblique case or a *lomeno* reading), exactly as for *milion*.
- NTS-42 D (*lomeno*/*lomené* under an oblique case) and E (zero in an oblique case).
- UK from 10^12: same mechanism available, paradigm audit not done (kept in TODO, NTS-42).

## Dump

The NTS-44 dump (74 converters × every variant combination: cardinals, ordinals, fractions, multiplicatives, decimals,
`Number`, years, clock) extended with the generated scales: 47 cardinals from 10^12 to 10^6024 (each family, multipliers
1–5, 21, 22, 101, compounds of two scales), 25 numerators and 12 denominators from 10^12. Base `b4a3e189` (git worktree)
vs branch, same inputs: **736 143 lines, 21 890 differ**, all in `CS`, `CS-CZ` (5 968 each), `SK`, `SK-SK` (4 977
each). No other language, no ordinal, multiplicative, decimal, year or clock line changes; no line goes from a reading to
an exception.

| Cause | Lines | Check |
|---|---|---|
| NTS-36 respelling, cardinals | 1 596 | the after text equals the before text with the Latin components respelled (*quadri* → *kvadri* … , script), 0 exception |
| NTS-36 respelling, fractions (nominative numerators read by the cardinal pipeline) | 774 | same check |
| NTS-42 F, exception → reading | 19 520 | below |

All 19 520 opened lines have a numerator or a denominator from 10^12; no fraction whose two terms are below 10^12
changes (10^12 − 1 over any denominator below 10^12 is byte-identical).
Oracle: each opened reading whose large side is one generated scale (any multiplier, a remainder below 10^6) was
recomputed independently as the reading of the same fraction on *milion*/*milión* (*-on* family) or *miliarda* (*-arda*
family), whose paradigms the plugins already held, with only the stem swapped: **8 986 lines checked, 0 mismatch**.
The 10 534 others (a scale on both sides, compounds of two scales such as 10^12 + 10^6, 2 × 10^12 + 5 × 10^9,
10^15 + 10^12, denominators that would land on a fraction noun) were reviewed by pattern: every part declined in CS
(*jednomu bilionu jednomu milionu třetin*, *dvěma bilionům pěti miliardám třetin*, *jedné biliardě jednomu bilionu
třetin*), the last component only in SK (*jeden bilión jednému miliónu tretín*, *dva bilióny piatimi miliardami
tretín*, *jedna biliarda jednému biliónu tretín*), the nominative numerator through the cardinal pipeline before a
*lomeno*/*lomené* denominator (*jeden bilion jeden milion lomeno jedním bilionem*). Lines still closed after the change,
as intended: the CS families A and B, the SK genitive singular of a generated *-ón* noun (also when it is the last
component of a compound: 10^15 + 10^12), D and E.
