# NTS-52 — the connector between a currency numeral and its noun (2026-10-23)

NTS-52 is closed. Starting master: `ec65069e` (NTS-50/51, #657). Sources: `docs/NTS-08-linguistic-sources.md`, section
NTS-52. Opened: NTS-53 (cardinal defects observed by the dump: CA `milións`, CA/GL `un mil milió(n)`, RO `unu euro`).

## Architecture — `<Currency><NounConnector>`

One declarative abstraction for the text between the numeral of a currency amount (unit or subunit) and the noun it
governs; it replaces the growth of ad hoc attributes (`terminalScaleConnector` was the first, an elided form and a
count-based form would have been the next).

```xml
<Currency>
    <NounConnector default="" nounSeparator="…">
        <Substitution value="de" nounSeparator="…" terminalScales="2.." numberRange="20.." numberModulo="100"
                      beforeNoun="a,e,i,o,u" />
    </NounConnector>
    <Replacement … />
</Currency>
```

- **Default.** `default` (empty or absent: none) and its `nounSeparator`. An empty default without substitution gives
  the historical text: `numeral + Separator + noun`, never two spaces.
- **Substitutions.** `value` (required; empty: no connector, which cancels a non-empty default), `nounSeparator`
  (absent: the language's `Separator`; `""` after an elided `d'`), and one to three constraints, all of which must hold:
  - `terminalScales`: the scale noun the numeral ends on, through the NTS-51 structural `GetTerminalScaleIndex` (not
    duplicated): the scale of the lowest non-zero group, a paired multiplier being one group at its named scale;
  - `numberRange`: the count of the amount, each of the unit and the subunit on its own count; `numberModulo` (at least
    2) tests the range on the count modulo that value, as a modifier of `numberRange`, not a fourth constraint;
  - `beforeNoun`: comma-separated beginnings of the noun, `OrdinalIgnoreCase`; no linguistic category is inferred.
- **Resolution.** The matching substitutions are collected; the one with the most constraints (`Specificity`: 1 to 3;
  neither `value` nor `nounSeparator` counts) wins; at equal specificity the first declared wins — the declaration
  order is authoritative, never an ambiguity error; with no match, the default.
- **Assembly.** `numeral + Separator + noun` when the selected connector is empty, otherwise `numeral + Separator +
  connector + nounSeparator + noun`. One helper, `BuildCurrencyNounJunction`; nothing re-reads the rendered numeral and
  no language identifier or word is tested in code (`beforeNoun` reads the noun, which is its declared meaning).
- **Pipeline.** Variants (caller < `forceVariants` < `CurrencyDefinition`), cardinal, currency replacements, separator,
  junction, noun — unchanged otherwise. Nothing outside `ConvertCurrency` changed: no `ConvertRaw`, cardinal grammar,
  scale form or ordinal.

### Public API (additive)

- `CurrencyNounConnector` (immutable: `Default`, `DefaultNounSeparator`, `Substitutions` snapshotted in the order
  supplied) and `CurrencyNounConnectorRule` (immutable: `Value`, `NounSeparator`, `TerminalScales`, `NumberRange`,
  `NumberModulo`, `BeforeNoun`, `Specificity`), validated at construction: white-space value, empty range, scale below
  1, negative count, modulo below 2 or without range or leaving no residue in the range, empty noun beginning, and a
  substitution without constraint (the default covers that case) are rejected.
- `NumberToStringConverterOptions.CurrencyNounConnector`, `NumberToStringConverter.CurrencyNounConnector` (the
  configured instance, reused as is since it is immutable; copied by the options copy constructor).
- XML model: `CurrencyType.NounConnector`, `CurrencyNounConnectorType`, `CurrencyNounConnectorSubstitutionType`. XSD:
  `NounConnector` before the `Replacement` children; patterns reject `terminalScales="0.."`, `"..2"`, malformed ranges,
  a negative or malformed `numberRange`, `numberModulo` below 2, an empty `beforeNoun` or entry; unknown attributes and
  children are rejected by the schema.

### NTS-51 compatibility

- The public `CurrencyTerminalScaleConnector` / `CurrencyTerminalScales` (options, converter, `CurrencyType`, XSD
  attributes) are kept (option A): the converter synthesizes exactly one substitution `terminalScales=<range>
  value=<connector>` with the standard separator. They are not reported as `CurrencyNounConnector`, so cloning a
  converter never sets both models; setting both is rejected (`ArgumentException`, XML or options).
- ES and IT are migrated to `<NounConnector default=""><Substitution terminalScales="2.." value="de|di" />`: their dump
  is byte-identical to master. The Security test `CurrencyPhrase_SourceMutationAfterConstruction_DoesNotChangeConverter`
  pinned the Spanish legacy getter (`"de"`), which is now `null` by this migration: it was corrected to pin the
  connector snapshot instead, with the same immutability invariant (and an extra mutation of the options' connector).

## FR — *de* / *d'* (FR, FR-fr, FR-ca, FR-be, FR-ch)

Académie française (9th ed., "million": *plusieurs millions d'euros*; "milliard": *plusieurs milliards de francs, de
livres, d'euros*) and OQLF (*suivis par la préposition de (ou par la forme élidée d')*; *cinq milliards cinq cent
cinquante et un millions de dollars* but *cinq millions deux cent quarante-deux mille cent un litres*).

```xml
<NounConnector default="">
    <Substitution terminalScales="2.." value="de" />
    <Substitution terminalScales="2.." beforeNoun="a,à,â,æ,e,é,è,ê,ë,i,î,ï,o,ô,œ,u,ù,û,ü" value="d'" nounSeparator="" />
</NounConnector>
```

| Amount | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| 1 000 000 euros | un-million euros | un-million d'euros |
| 2 000 000 euros | deux-millions euros | deux-millions d'euros |
| 1 000 000 / 2 000 000 francs | un-million francs, … | un-million de francs, deux-millions de francs |
| 2 500 000 000 euros | deux-milliards-cinq-cents-millions euros | deux-milliards-cinq-cents-millions d'euros |
| 1 000 000 écus / yens | un-million écus, … | un-million d'écus, un-million de yens |
| 1 200 000 / 1 000 001 / 1 001 000 euros | — | unchanged (un-million-deux-cent-mille euros, …) |

*h* is not listed (mute and aspirated *h* cannot be told apart from a caller-defined noun: a noun in *h* keeps *de*),
nor *y* (*de yens*). FR-be and FR-ch inherit the element through `baseOn` (FR-ca shares the file); the NTS-49 hyphenated
numeral is unchanged. A feminine currency keeps the masculine noun (*un-million de livres*, *une livre*).

## CA — *de* / *d'* (CA, ca-ES, ca-ES-valencia)

UOC, "Escriptura dels numerals" (*els numerals milió, bilió, trilió i quadrilió s'enllacen amb el substantiu […]
mitjançant la preposició de*; *dos milions d'euros*) and "L'apòstrof" (*de s'apostrofa davant de totes les vocals
(també precedides de h muda)*; *no s'apostrofa […] davant de i i u consonants: de iens, de iod*); UB, Llibre d'estil.

```xml
<NounConnector default="">
    <Substitution terminalScales="2.." value="de" />
    <Substitution terminalScales="2.." beforeNoun="ia,ie,io,iu,ua,ue,uo,hia,hie,hio,hiu,hua,hue,huo" value="de" />
    <Substitution terminalScales="2.." beforeNoun="a,à,e,è,é,i,í,ï,o,ò,ó,u,ú,ü,h" value="d'" nounSeparator="" />
</NounConnector>
```

The consonantal onsets and the elision both have two constraints; the consonantal ones are declared first and win:
*un milió de iens*, *un milió de hienes*, *un milió de uombats*, but *un milió d'hores*, *un milió d'euros*, *un milió de
pessetes*. Catalan *h* is mute in native words, so it is listed (unlike French). Valencian inherits through `baseOn`.
The graphic onset *io* also covers the rare vocalic *ió* (documented limitation).

## GL — *de* (GL, gl-ES)

RAG, "millón": *Tocoulle un millón de euros no sorteo*. `<Substitution terminalScales="2.." value="de" />`, never
elided: *un millón de euros*, *dous millóns de euros*; *un millón douscentos mil euros*, *un millón un euros* unchanged.

## RO — *de* by count (RO, RO-RO)

GALR 2005, vol. I, pp. 296–297: no *de* after 1–19 "and the compounds with these numerals in last position" (*o sută
treisprezece cărți*, *două milioane trei sute opt lei*), *de* after the compounds from twenty (*douăzeci de cărți*, *cinci
mii două sute de lei*). The rule is therefore not "≥ 20": `de` ⇔ n ≥ 20 and (n mod 100) ∉ 1..19. A plain range cannot
express the last two digits; the user chose `numberModulo` (a modifier of `numberRange`) over an approximation or
leaving RO out.

```xml
<NounConnector default="">
    <Substitution numberRange="1..19" numberModulo="100" value="" />
    <Substitution numberRange="20.." value="de" />
</NounConnector>
```

| Count | After |
|---|---|
| 2, 19, 101, 119, 1001, 1 000 001, 2 000 308 | no *de* (doi euro, o sută unu euro, două milioane trei sute opt euro) |
| 20, 21, 99, 100, 120, 1000, 5200, 1 000 000 | *de* (douăzeci de euro, o sută de euro, cinci mii două sute de euro, un milion de euro) |
| 2,20 lei / 20,02 lei | doi lei și douăzeci de bani / douăzeci de lei și doi bani |

The rule is distinct from `scaleConnector`, which acts inside the numeral (*douăzeci de mii*): both apply,
*douăzeci de mii de euro*. DOOM 3 could not be fetched; GALR is the Academy grammar.

## PT — audit only

Priberam, "milhão": *nome masculino*, *um milhão de joules*. The rule is attested, but PT declares `maxNumber="999999"`:
no unit amount can end on *milhão* (`ArgumentOutOfRangeException` from 1 000 000), so nothing is configured and the PT
dump is byte-identical. Opening the Portuguese million is outside NTS-52.

### Observations outside NTS-52

- NTS-53 (TODO): CA `dos milións` (UOC: *dos milions*), CA/GL `un mil milió(n)` for 10^9, RO `unu euro` before a noun and
  `o sută unu de mii` (against GALR a).
- `Utils.Range.IntRange<long>` (core): `Except` and the complement return an empty range when a bound is open below
  (`(..5) − (0..)` gives `[]`), and `"∞--1"` parses as `[1]`. `CurrencyNounConnectorRule` checks negatives through the
  intersection with `..0`, which works; the core bug is not fixed here.
