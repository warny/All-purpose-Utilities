# NTS-33 — French ordinals derived from base forms; NTS-26 closed by documentation (2026-10-22)

Starting master: `339fe665` (NTS-47, #654). Two tickets:

| Ticket | Result |
|---|---|
| NTS-33 — French ordinals keep the plural of the agreed cardinal | **fixed** (engine primitive + FR configuration) |
| NTS-26 — Wolof genitive *-i* inside nested multipliers | **closed by documentation / attested variation; current output retained intentionally** |

Only NTS-33 changes behaviour, and only French ordinals.

## NTS-33

### Root cause

The declarative ordinal pipeline (plugin → exceptions → `OrdinalComposition` → `OrdinalScale` → cardinal →
variants → ordinal `Replacement`s → word rules / `OrdinalStem` / `removeTrailing` → suffix) transformed the
**agreed standalone cardinal**. French agrees *cent* and the scale nouns in the cardinal (`deux cents`,
`deux millions`), so the suffix was appended to the plural: `deux centsième`, `trois cents millionsième`,
`deux millionsième`, `dix milliardsième`, `deux billionsième`. The configuration already held the composition form
`deux cent *` (`buildString`), but not the form of a hundred standing alone as the base of a derivation.

Three levels must be kept apart:

| Level | 200 | 2 000 000 |
|---|---|---|
| cardinal (agreed) | `deux cents` | `deux millions` |
| derivational base | `deux cent` | `deux` + singular `million` |
| ordinal | `deux centième` | `deux millionième` |
| fraction noun, agreed with its numerator | `deux centièmes` (2/100) | `deux millionièmes` (2/1 000 000) |

Sources (full quotations in `docs/NTS-08-linguistic-sources.md`, section "NTS-33"): OQLF *Écriture des fractions*
(cardinals *deux cents*; ordinals *centième, deux centième, trois centième*, "le trois centième candidat (300e)";
fractions *un centième, les trois centièmes*), OQLF *Pluriel de vingt, de cent et de mille* (*vingt* and *cent*
invariable with an ordinal value: *la page deux cent*, *la page trois cent quatre-vingt*), CCDMD numeral list (rank
*la deux-millionième fois*, *le six-milliardième habitant*; fractions *deux millionièmes*, *six milliardièmes*),
Larousse *millionième* (adj. num. ord.).

### Why not ordinal `Replacement`s

`cents → cent`, `millions → million` with `scope="Anywhere"` would fix the five recorded values but also rewrite the
leading cardinal groups of non-round ordinals (`deux millions unième` → `deux million unième`). The fix is
structural and limited to the constituent the ordinal is derived from.

### Primitive: `ordinalBaseString`

- XML: optional `ordinalBaseString` on `<Digit>` (XSD `DigitType`, `minLength="1"`, documented); public
  `DigitType.OrdinalBaseString` (additive API).
- Semantics: used instead of `string` (and of `innerString`) when the digit stands alone at the end of the constituent
  an ordinal is derived from. That constituent is the **lowest group** of a cardinal-based ordinal, or the **whole
  multiplier** of an `<OrdinalScale>` value. Never used by `Convert`, fractions, decimals, currencies, years, times or
  multiplicatives; `buildString` is unchanged; leading groups keep their cardinal form. Absent: historical behaviour.
  An empty value fails the schema, a blank one programmatically throws `ArgumentException`.
- Snapshot: copied by `CopyDigit` (default and scale-scoped tables, `BuildGroupTable`, the exposed `Groups`, the
  options clone); conversions never read the mutable DTO. `baseOn` follows the `Groups` rule (a child redeclaring
  its groups replaces them, base included); no parallel inheritance.
- Rendering: an `ordinalBase` flag on `ConvertRaw` / `ConvertGroup`, true only for the lowest group; a digit
  without lower text then returns its base. The cardinal path passes `false` and is byte-identical. No duplication
  of `ConvertRaw`, no second scale-noun property.
- `OrdinalScale`: `TryBuildRoundScaleText` renders the multiplier with `ordinalBase: true` (`trois cent` + `million`
  → `trois cent millionième`), never as an ordinal of its own. Without bases configured, its output is unchanged.

Not added (no demonstrated need): an `ordinalBaseBuildString`, since a hundred followed by a lower part already
composes through `buildString` (`deux cent *`), and a scale-noun base, since `OrdinalScale` already takes the singular
form through `ResolveScaleWord(index, 1, …)`.

### French configuration

- FR, FR-be, FR-ch level-3 digits 2–9: `ordinalBaseString="deux cent"` … `"neuf cent"` (FR-be and FR-ch redeclare
  their `Groups`). FR-be level-2 digit 8: `ordinalBaseString="quatre-vingt"` (`cent quatre-vingtième`, was
  `cent quatre-vingtsième`).
- FR `<OrdinalScale scales="2..6" multiplierSeparator=" " />` (inherited by FR-be and FR-ch): million to trillion,
  every scale a `long` reaches (10^18; `long.MaxValue` ≈ 9.2 × 10^18). Scale 1 (*mille*) stays on the cardinal path,
  which already gives the invariable *mille* (`deux millième`); its multiplier agreement is the cardinal defect NTS-48.
- FR `<GeneratedScaleForm suffix="0|1">` with `forceVariants="gender=masculin"`: the round-scale path applies the
  variant rules to the multiplier alone, so without it the feminine gave `vingt et une millionième`; the nouns are
  masculine (OQLF: "Million est un nom"). The cardinals are byte-identical (the whole-number feminine pass already
  only touched the last word).
- Fractions, decimals, cardinals, variants and the `<Fractions>` table are untouched.

### Found, not fixed — NTS-48

FR and FR-be write `deux cents mille` (200 000) for *deux cent mille*; FR writes `quatre-vingt` (80) for
*quatre-vingts*; FR-be `quatre-vingts mille`. These are cardinal defects (NTS-33 keeps cardinals byte-identical);
the ordinals built on them inherit them (`deux cents millième`). Opened as NTS-48.

## NTS-26

No output change. The nested Wolof multipliers keep the inner genitive *-i* (`ñaari téeméeri milyoŋ`, `juróom ñenti
téeméeri milyoŋ`, `ñaari téeméeri junni`): Guérin (CNRS/LLACAN) analyses them as genitive constructions
(*ñaar-i téeméer-i dërëm*); the form without the inner linker is attested as well (Wolof Ajami Reader, Ngom et al.,
Boston University 2025, p. 192: *juróom ñaar téeméeri milyoŋ*; Gaye 1980; Kosogorova 2023); decree 2005-992 sets the
general *-u/-i* linker but not these chains. Variation, not a defect; changing the default would rewrite many
byte-stable Wolof outputs. No variant dimension. Documented in `docs/NTS-26-wolof-nested-linker-choice.md` (question,
current form, evidence for and against, decree, decision, reopening criteria), in a comment of
`NumberConvertionConfiguration.WO.xml`, and pinned by `NumberToStringNts21Tests.Wolof_NestedLinker_Nts26_RetainedByDecision`.
