# NTS-30F — Spanish and Basque million multipliers of four to six digits (2026-10-15)

ES/EU item of NTS-30. Starting master: `bde75adc` (NTS-37, #641).
Sources: `docs/NTS-08-linguistic-sources.md`, section NTS-30F.

## Root cause

ES and EU named 10^9 as an independent scale (`<Scale value="3" string="mil millones" />`,
`<Scale value="3" string="mila milioi" />`). Any number of millions above 999 was split between that scale and the
millions: `dos mil millones quinientos millones` for *dos mil quinientos millones*, `mil millones un millón` for
*mil un millones*, EU `bi mila milioi eta bostehun milioi` for *bi mila eta bostehun milioi*. In both languages the
multiplier of *millón*/*milioi* is one cardinal of up to six digits (DPD "números": *13 000 millones*; Euskaltzaindia
rule 203: *berrehun eta sei mila zazpiehun eta laurogeita hamabost milioi*).

## Configuration

No engine change: the NTS-37 primitive `pairedScalesFrom` expresses both languages.

- ES and EU: `<NumberScale pairedScalesFrom="2">`. Scale 2 takes the group above it into its multiplier, rendered as
  a complete cardinal; scale 3 is the unnamed upper half of the pair (`CanNameGroup(3)` false, `GetScaleName(3)`
  throws `InvalidOperationException`). *mil millones* / *mila milioi* are now generated structurally: the multiplier
  1000 renders *mil* / *mila* through the existing thousands rule (`onScale="1" onValue="1"`), then the scale noun.
- ES removed: `<Scale value="3" string="mil millones" />`, `uno mil millones → mil millones` (onScale 3, onValue 1),
  `uno mil millones → un mil millones` (onScale 3); `veintiuno mil → veintiún mil` narrowed from `onScale="1,3"` to
  `"1"`. The NTS-27 apocope rules on scales 1 and 2 now also apply inside and after the paired multiplier: *mil un
  millones*, *veintiún mil un millones*, *ciento veintiún mil ciento veintiún millones*.
- EU removed: `<Scale value="3" string="mila milioi" />` and `bat mila milioi → mila milioi` (onScale 3, onValue 1).
  `bat milioi → milioi bat` keeps `onValue="1"`: the inversion stays limited to one million, so a multiplier ending in
  one keeps its order (*mila eta bat milioi*). The multiplier's own *eta* comes from the existing `groupConnector`
  and the `eta …ehun eta` replacements (*mila eta bostehun milioi*, *bi mila bostehun eta bat milioi*).
- `maxNumber` unchanged (999 999 999 999): no *billón* / *bilioi*; 10^12 still throws `ArgumentOutOfRangeException`.

## Regression dump

All 74 converters against `bde75adc` (dump extended with an NTS-30F section: million multipliers 1 to 999 999 with
lower groups, negatives, variants, decimals, currency, fractions, `Number`, ordinals from 999 999 999), 1 745 656
lines: **331 changed, in ES (121), EU (105) and eu-ES (105) only**.

- Every changed line has a value ≥ 10^9 (cardinal, negative, decimal integer part, currency amount, fraction
  numerator or denominator, `Number`), except `GetScaleName(3)`, which now throws (one line per culture).
- No line changed below 10^9; no changed line was an error before.
- Ordinals byte-identical (the guards still close 10^9 and above); clock (`K`) and times (`T`) byte-identical;
  `MathematicalFraction` reading still unsupported in ES/EU (unchanged exception).
- ES feminine variants change exactly as the masculine ones (`dos mil millones quinientas millones` →
  `dos mil quinientas millones`): the feminine hundreds before *millones* predate this ticket and are recorded under
  NTS-30.

## Found, not fixed

- NTS-30 (EU): an *eta* after *milioi* before a non-final component (`hirurogei milioi eta berrehun mila eta bat`
  vs rule 203 *hirurogei milioi berrehun mila eta bat*), below and above 10^9.
- NTS-30 (ES): feminine hundreds in a multiplier of *millones* (`quinientas millones`).
- NTS-38 (opened): EU ordinal *milioigarren* (rule 203) for the current `milioi batgarren`; ES/EU ordinals from 10^9.
- The ES currency apocope (`uno euro`) stays a separate NTS-30 item.
