# NTS-30H — Ukrainian, Bulgarian, Czech and Slovak fractions (2026-10-15)

The NTS-30 item "UK, BG, CS, SK `ConvertFraction`" (seen by NTS-30B). Starting master: `7eb86074` (NTS-30G, #643).
Sources: `docs/NTS-08-linguistic-sources.md`, section NTS-30H.

## Root cause

`ConvertFraction` and the fraction form of `Convert(Number)` read every language as *numerator,
`fractionSeparator`, denominator cardinal*. UK, BG, CS and SK declared `на` / `ku`, which produced `один на тисяча`,
`едно на хиляда`, `jedna ku tisíc`: none of these languages reads a fraction that way. UK and BG read the denominator
as an ordinal agreeing with the numerator (Правопис 2019 §107: *одна третя*, *дві третіх*; ИБЕ-БАН: *една втора*,
*две трети*); CS and SK read it as a fraction noun (*jedna třetina*, *dvě třetiny*, *pět třetin*; *jedna tretina*).
Neither shape is expressible by the declarative configuration.

## Engine — `IFractionLanguageSpecifics`

New public extension point (`IFractionLanguageSpecifics.cs`), implemented by the language's `<LanguageSpecifics>`
instance: `TryGetFractionDenominator(numerator, denominator, variants, out FractionDenominatorReading?)` returns the
denominator text in the form the numerator governs and the variants it forces on the numerator
(`FractionDenominatorReading`, immutable, non-blank text).

`BuildFractionText` keeps every other step: named power-of-ten `<Fractions>` keep precedence, the
zero-denominator rejection and the move of the sign to the numerator happen before, the numerator goes through the
cardinal pipeline (`BuildCardinalFragment`) with the forced variants (canonicalized, `UNTS006` on an unknown
dimension or value) overlaid on the caller's query, one finalization, the outer sign. `false` throws
`NotSupportedException`: no fallback to the connector reading.

## Languages

- UK (`UkrainianOrdinalLanguageSpecifics`): feminine numerator; the ordinal the plugin forms, feminine nominative
  singular after one, genitive plural otherwise (`-их`/`-іх`); compound denominators keep their prefix
  (*дві двадцять п'ятих*, *одна сто перша*).
- BG (`BulgarianOrdinalLanguageSpecifics`): feminine numerator; feminine singular ordinal after one, plural
  (`-ен → -ни`) otherwise; *една четвърт*.
- CS (`CzechOrdinalLanguageSpecifics`): IJP fraction nouns 2–12, 100, 1000, 10^6, by numerator class
  one / two to four / five and more.
- SK (`SlovakOrdinalLanguageSpecifics`): *polovica*, *tretina* (with *tretiny*, *tretín*), *pätina*, *stotina*,
  *tisícina*; the plurals the sources do not give are absent.
- Shared helpers on `SlavicAdjectivalOrdinalLanguageSpecifics` (numerator classification, nominative check, noun
  lexicon reading).
- `fractionSeparator` removed from the four XML configurations (no other surface used it; no `baseOn` child).

Fail closed: compound numerators ending in *один/една* after the tens (UK, BG) or in 1–4 (CS, SK), zero numerators,
denominator one, denominators the ordinal plugin declines or without a sourced noun, non-nominative cases (UK, CS,
SK). Opened as NTS-39.
